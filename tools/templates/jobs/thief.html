HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most Thief guides on the web describe the game after 2009: Bully, Despoil, Conspirator, Collaborator, Rudra's Storm, Treasure Hunter on half your gear and level-99 Dual Wield. None of that is on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with its era-revert modules applied. Ability values, Treasure Hunter math, weapon skill numbers, skill caps, gear stats and drops all come from that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki, because the server code can't tell you where to stand or what an NPC says.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves time, TP or gil
  • WARNING — a common way to die or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • MASTER THIEF'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — three things this guide can't settle from the code.

  • The Treasure Hunter drop table in Phoenix's code is the 2017 retail table (scripts/combat/basic/treasure_hunter.lua). Phoenix doesn't revert it. That's how the server works today, but it isn't the 2007 table.
  • Assassin's Charge lasts up to 60 seconds in the code and only ends early when you weapon skill. Test it before you plan around it.
  • Live server settings such as the drop-rate multiplier and whether Treasure Hunter procs are switched off aren't public. The numbers here are the code defaults (procs on, multiplier 1.0).

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE THIEF

The tank holds it. The mages heal it. You rob it blind.

On Phoenix a THF does four things, in this order of importance:

  1. Raise the drop rate. Treasure Hunter makes every mob you touch drop more. Linkshells bring a Thief for that alone.
  2. Burst with Sneak Attack and Trick Attack. A guaranteed critical hit with your DEX or AGI added, then a weapon skill on top.
  3. Pull and get out. Flee, Hide, and the best evasion skill in the game.
  4. Steal and Mug. Free items and gil from every mob.

Job abilities on Phoenix (era-reverted)

FROM THE CODE — Sneak Attack and Trick Attack. Both only work with Thief as your main job, and only on the first hit of the round (attack.cpp).

  • Sneak Attack needs you behind the mob (or Hidden). It adds your full DEX to the base damage and sets crit rate to 100% (battleutils.cpp).
  • Trick Attack needs a party member between you and the mob. It adds your full AGI to the damage. At level 60 the Assassin trait makes Trick Attack hits auto-hit and auto-crit too.
  • Use both together (SATA): one hit gets DEX + AGI, can't miss, crits, and sends the hate to the tank.

FROM THE CODE — the era Assassin's Charge and Feint. Phoenix's era module (modules/era/lua/globals/job_utils/thief.lua) removes the Quadruple Attack from Assassin's Charge (Triple Attack +100% only), and removes the Treasure Hunter boost from Feint. The only thing that ends Assassin's Charge early in the code is a weapon skill.

CHAPTER 5

THIEF MATH (TREASURE HUNTER, STEAL, MUG AND SATA)

Every number in this chapter is the reason a linkshell invites you.

Treasure Hunter: how it actually works on Phoenix

FROM THE CODE — the three rules. 1. Starting level. The first time you act on a mob (hit it, Steal, debuff it), the mob's TH level becomes your TH from traits and gear, up to TH8 if Thief is your main job, TH4 if it isn't (enmity_container.cpp). 2. Procs. Every first hit of a round from a main-job Thief has a chance to raise the mob's TH by 1, up to TH12 (battleutils.cpp). The chance is 4% when the mob is at your TH level and halves for each level above it. Sneak Attack and Trick Attack each make that hit 10× more likely to proc (both together: 20×). 3. Drops. When the mob dies, each drop's rate is replaced by the value in the TH table for its rarity and the mob's TH level (scripts/combat/basic/treasure_hunter.lua).

What TH does to drop rates (from Phoenix's table):

Mob's TH Very common Common Uncommon Rare Very rare
0 24% 15% 10% 5% 1%
1 48% 30% 12% 6% 1.5%
2 56% 40% 15% 7% 2%
3 60% 42.5% 16.5% 7.5% 2.25%
4 64% 45% 18% 8% 2.5%
6 68% 50% 20% 9% 3.5%
8 70.5% 55% 22.5% 10.5% 4.75%
10 73.5% 60% 26.5% 12.5% 6.5%
12 76% 65% 29.5% 15.5% 8.25%

TH1 doubles common drops. Going from TH4 to TH12 roughly doubles rare and very rare drops again.

Where your TH comes from on Phoenix:

Source TH How
Treasure Hunter I (Lv15) +1 Trait
Treasure Hunter II (Lv45) +1 Trait
Thief's Knife (Lv70 dagger) +1 Always drops from Sozu Rogberry, Temple of Uggalepih

That's TH4 to start a mob at 75. Everything above that comes from procs.

PRO TIP — SATA builds TH. A hit with both Sneak Attack and Trick Attack is 20× more likely to proc TH. On a notorious monster, open with SATA every minute (both recasts are 1:00) and use /th (Phoenix's command) to check the mob's TH level.

FROM THE CODE — Phoenix's /th command. Phoenix adds a player command, /th, that prints the TH level of the mob you're targeting (modules/phoenix/lua/commands/th.lua). Use it to see your procs.

WARNING — TH only counts while you're on the hate list. You have to act on the mob (a hit, Steal, a debuff). A Thief who sits back and never touches the NM adds nothing.

NOT ON PHOENIX (DON'T FARM THESE)

WARNING — these show up in "best THF gear" lists, but you can't get them here.

  • Zeni Notorious Monster drops — Enkidu's Harness and the rest of the set, Skadi's pieces. Phoenix hides the ZNM pop NPCs and spawn points (modules/phoenix/lua/zones/npc_visibility.lua).
  • Einherjar rewards — the Einherjar NPC is hidden.
  • Homam set — Salvage gear. Phoenix has no Salvage; the only source in the code is a later (Rhapsodies) vendor.
  • White Rarab Cap +1 (TH+1) — no drop or quest gives it on Phoenix.
  • Byakko's Haidate — real, but Thief can't wear them.

MASTER THIEF'S CHALLENGE — Sky. Thieves are the reason Sky linkshells get rich. Tag every Sky god and pop NM with TH4, SATA it every minute, and push it toward TH8: a "rare" 5% drop becomes 10.5% (Chapter 5's table).

CHAPTER 8

QUESTS, AF AND ENDGAME

Rogue's armor is built for thieving, not fighting. Get it anyway — the Poulaines are the best Steal piece until relic.

AF1 · The Tenshodo Showdown (level 40) → Marauder's Knife

You need to be a Tenshodo member first: Tenshodo Membership from Ghebi Damomohe in Lower Jeuno (needs Jeuno fame 3).

  1. Talk to Nanaa Mihgo (Windurst Woods, J-3) as a level 40+ Thief. She gives you the Letter from the Tenshodo.
  2. Take it to Harnek in the Tenshodo (Lower Jeuno). He gives you the Tenshodo Envelope.
  3. Go to Elfriede at the Selbina inn. She wants a Bowl of Quadav Stew.
  4. Steal the stew from Bronze, Silver, Zircon or Garnet Quadav in Beadeaux. Bring Sneak and Invisible; Beadeaux Quadav detect by sound.
  5. Trade the stew to Elfriede for the Signed Envelope, then return to Harnek.

PRO TIP — the stew is a Steal-only item. Killing Quadav won't drop it. Wear any Steal+ you have.

AF2 · As Thick as Thieves (level 50) → Rogue's Bonnet

  1. Nanaa Mihgo gives you Gang Whereabouts and two forged envelopes.
  2. Sauromugue Champaign: check the ??? at the tower to spawn Climbpix Highrise. Kill it for the Grapnel.
  3. Go to the tower at I/J-7, remove all your gear (weapons too), and trade the Grapnel to the ??? to climb. You get the first signed envelope.
  4. Lower Jeuno: talk to Sniggnix to start the dice games. In Dangruf Wadi, three goblins want items:
  5. Saltvix — Rock Salt
  6. Grasswix — Gausebit Wildgrass
  7. Eggblix — Lizard Egg
  8. In North Gustaberg, check the ??? to spawn Gambilox Wanderling. Kill it for the Regal Die.
  9. Trade the Regal Die to Sniggnix for the second signed envelope, then return to Nanaa.

WARNING — strip to climb. The climb fails if anything is equipped. Unequip everything, including your weapons and ammo, before trading the Grapnel.

AF3 · Hitting the Marquisate (level 50) → Rogue's Poulaines

  1. Nanaa Mihgo gives you the Cat Burglar's Note.
  2. Yatniel (Lower Jeuno) wants 4 Quake Grenades (Alchemy 58 or Smithing 29 sub, or the AH).
  3. Hagain (Mhaura) gives you the Bomb Incense.
  4. Garlaige Citadel: use the incense on 8 ??? in order. The last one spawns Chandelier. Hide, then claim it; it drops Chandelier Coal.
  5. Trade the coal to Hagain, then talk to Nanaa.
  6. Go through Ordelle's Caves to the hidden La Theine Plateau valley (H-10) and trade a Pickaxe to the ???.

Reward: Rogue's Poulaines (Steal+2, Flee duration+) and the title Paragon of Thief Excellence.

Borghertz's Sneaky Hands (level 50, after AF2) → Rogue's Armlets

  1. Talk to Guslam in Upper Jeuno as a level 50+ Thief who has finished As Thick as Thieves.
  2. Open a Treasure Coffer in Monastic Cavern to get the Old Gauntlets key item.
  3. Report back to Guslam. On your first Borghertz quest you then talk to Deadly Minnow (Upper Jeuno) and pay Yin Pocanakhu (Lower Jeuno) 1,000 gil; later Borghertz quests skip this step.
  4. Check the ??? in Port Jeuno to meet Borghertz; he asks for Shadow Flames.
  5. In Castle Zvahl Baileys, check the Torch to spawn Dark Spark. Kill it, check the Torch again for Shadow Flames.
  6. Return to the ??? in Port Jeuno.

FROM THE CODE — one Borghertz at a time. You can't start a second Borghertz quest while another is active (scripts/quests/jeuno/helpers.lua).

Coffers (only after you start Borghertz)

Piece Zone
Rogue's Culottes Castle Oztroja coffer
Rogue's Vest Castle Zvahl Baileys coffer

FROM THE CODE — the coffers wait for Borghertz. The AF legs and body only come out of these coffers once Borghertz's Sneaky Hands is started or done, and only on THF main (scripts/quests/jeuno/helpers.lua).

FROM THE CODE — tools on coffers. A main-job Thief can open coffers with Thief's Tools (65% success, 10% Mimic), Living Key (70%) or Skeleton Key (75%) instead of the zone key (scripts/globals/treasure.lua). Success drops 5% for every level the coffer is above you. Zone keys never fail.

Evisceration · Cloak and Dagger

  1. Talk to Jakoh Wahcondalo in Kazham with Dagger skill 230+. You get the Weapon Training Guide and a Dagger of Trials.
  2. Earn 300 weapon skill points with it equipped (5 per WS, +2/+4/+6 for skillchain levels 1/2/3).
  3. Trade the dagger back to Jakoh for the Map to the Annals of Truth.
  4. In Gustav Tunnel, check the ??? to spawn Baronial Bat. Kill it, then check the ??? again for the Annals of Truth.
  5. Return to Jakoh. Evisceration is yours.

FROM THE CODE — no merit weapon skills. Phoenix's weapon skill merit category has no upgrades, so Evisceration is your top WS for good.
