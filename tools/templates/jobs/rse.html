HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

RSE (Race Specific Equipment) is a four-piece set — body, hands, legs, feet — that only your race can wear. Every race gets a different set with different stats. They're level 27–33 pieces that every job can wear, and some of them stay useful well past 40.

This guide is built from Phoenix's own server code: the quest script, the code that picks whose week it is, and the treasure-chest script. The live schedule is on Phoenix's site:

CHAPTER 1

HOW RSE WORKS

Four pieces. One per trip. And the Goblin only sews for one race at a time.

The quest, step by step

  1. Talk to Guttrix in Lower Jeuno (level 10+, Jeuno fame 3). He tells you whose week it is and where. That starts The Goblin Tailor.
  2. Wait for your race's week (check the calendar). The week lasts 7 hours 41 minutes real time.
  3. Go to that week's dungeon with its chest key and find the Treasure Chest.
  4. Trade the key to the chest. Instead of normal loot you get the key item Magical Pattern.
  5. Bring it to Guttrix and pick one piece: body, hands, legs or feet.
  6. Repeat for each piece you want. You can only hold one Magical Pattern at a time, so it's one chest per piece.

FROM THE CODE — the exact checks. The_Goblin_Tailor.lua only gives the pattern when all three are true: the chest is in that week's dungeon, the week's race matches your character's race and gender exactly, and you don't already hold a pattern. The chest opens with the dungeon's key or (main-job Thief only) Thief's Tools, Living Key or Skeleton Key. It skips the chest's "illusion" check, so a recently opened chest still works once it reappears.

WARNING — male and female are separate weeks. Hume ♂ and Hume ♀ have different weeks, and so do Elvaan ♂/♀ and Tarutaru ♂/♀. A female Tarutaru can't use the male Tarutaru week, even though the gear is the same Wonder set. Galka and Mithra have one week each.

The rotation

FROM THE CODE — how the week is picked. src/common/vana_time.h counts Vana'diel weeks (8 game days = 7 h 40 m 48 s real time). The race is week mod 8, the dungeon is week mod 3.

The races go in this order, one per Vana'diel week, then repeat:

Order Race
1 Hume ♂
2 Hume ♀
3 Elvaan ♂
4 Elvaan ♀
5 Tarutaru ♂
6 Tarutaru ♀
7 Mithra
8 Galka

The dungeons go Ordelle's Caves → Gusgen Mines → Maze of Shakhrami, one per week, then repeat.

What that means for you:

  • Your race comes up every 8 weeks: about 61 hours 27 minutes (2½ real days).
  • Your dungeon shifts each time. Because 8 weeks is 2 steps around a 3-dungeon cycle, your race's dungeon goes Ordelle's → Shakhrami → Gusgen → Ordelle's…
  • The full pattern repeats every 24 weeks, about 7 days 16 hours.

PRO TIP — trust the calendar, not your math. The rotation is fixed, but the server's clock and any offset it uses decide where it starts. Phoenix's calendar shows the real current week: https://phoenix-xi.com/world-info?tab=calendar

WARNING — keys are Rare/Ex. You can carry one of each dungeon's key and you can't buy them on the AH. That's why you farm your key before the week starts.

PRO TIP — farm your key the day before. Look at the calendar, see which dungeon your race gets next, and farm that one key ahead of time. Then you spend your 7 h 41 m window walking chest to chest, not killing Goblins hoping for a drop. If you want more than one piece, farm a key for each trip: after you turn in a pattern, go straight back for the next key while the week is still on.

PRO TIP — bring a friend in their 30s or higher. The key mobs are level 25–36. At level 25 you'll want a party, or a higher-level friend to kill and let you lot the key.

PRO TIP — Thieves skip the key farm. A main-job Thief can use Thief's Tools, a Living Key or a Skeleton Key instead of a dungeon key. They work 75% of the time, fail 10% and trigger a trap (Weakness) 15%. Keys never fail.

Finding the chest

The chest has 12–16 possible spots per dungeon and moves after someone opens it, reappearing about 3 minutes later somewhere else. If the spot you remember is empty, someone beat you to it; check the next spot on your route.

CHECKPOINT — before your week

  • Jeuno fame 3 and The Goblin Tailor started with Guttrix.
  • The calendar checked: you know the start time and the dungeon.
  • That dungeon's chest key in your bag (or Thief's Tools on a THF).
  • A route through the dungeon's chest spots.

CHAPTER 4

WHICH JOBS WANT WHICH PIECES

Not every piece is worth a trip. These are.

Race Melee (WAR, MNK, THF, DRK, SAM, NIN, DRG, BST) Mage (WHM, BLM, RDM, SMN, BRD)
Hume Gloves (STR+3 DEX+3) to the mid-40s. Boots (AGI+3) for NIN/THF Slacks/Pants (MND+3, MP+32) for WHM/RDM. Boots (INT+3) for BLM
Elvaan Chausses (DEX+2 AGI+2) for THF/NIN Gauntlets (MP+24) and Ledelsens (MP+20)
Tarutaru The whole set. STR and HP where Taru are weakest Kaftan/Braccae for HP (survival), not stats
Mithra Gaiters (STR+3, CHR+2 for BST) Gauntlets (MND+2, VIT+4) and Loincloth (MP+32)
Galka Little; Sandals (AGI+3) for evasion Surcoat and Bracers (MP+32 each) — huge for a Galka mage

PRO TIP — Beastmasters, look for CHR. Custom Tunic/Vest (CHR+1), Savage Gaiters (CHR+2) and Elder's Braguette (CHR+2) help Charm.

PRO TIP — RSE is also a fashion set. The pieces look the same as other race-specific outfits and many players keep one for lockstyle later. If you're not sure you need it, a 7-hour window every 2½ days means you can come back anytime.
