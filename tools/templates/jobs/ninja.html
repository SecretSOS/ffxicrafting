HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most Ninja guides on the web describe the game after 2008: Yonin and Innin, Migawari, Utsusemi: San, Futae, Monomi and level 99 gear with Ninja Tool Expertise on every slot. None of that exists on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with the era-revert modules applied. Spell levels, tool costs, scroll prices, shadow rules, gear stats and drops all come from that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki and other quest sites, because the server code can't tell you where to stand or what an NPC says.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves shadows, tools or gil
  • WARNING — a common way to die or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • SHINOBI'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — two things this guide can't settle from the code.

  • Phoenix's code makes elemental ninjutsu stronger the higher your Ninjutsu skill is, but only when Ninja is your main job. I couldn't confirm that this rule existed in 2007. Chapter 5 shows the numbers with and without it.
  • The code gives a main-job Ninja 4 shadows from Utsusemi: Ni (3 if Ninja is your subjob). I couldn't find a source dating the 4-shadow rule, so treat it as "how Phoenix works", not "how 2007 worked". Count your shadows in-game: the icon on your buff bar changes with each one.

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE NINJA

Every other tank absorbs the hit. You make it miss.

On Phoenix a NIN does four things, in this order of importance:

  1. Keep shadows up. Utsusemi makes copies of you that soak hits. Shadows are your armor, your enmity tool and your safety net.
  2. Hit hard with two katanas. Dual Wield is built into the job from level 10.
  3. Hold hate when you're the tank: Provoke from /WAR, elemental ninjutsu, enfeebling ninjutsu.
  4. Debuff and pull. Slow (Hojo), Blind (Kurayami), Paralyze (Jubaku) and Invisible (Tonko) cost a tool each and no MP.

CHAPTER 2

BECOMING A NINJA

Ninja is an advanced job. You need any job at level 30, a trip to Korroloka Tunnel, and a boat ride's worth of patience.

The unlock quest — Ayame and Kaede (Port Bastok)

Requirements: any job at level 30+ (Phoenix's code uses the default ADVANCED_JOB_LEVEL = 30).

  1. Kaede — Port Bastok (J-5), the northernmost house. Talk to her for a cutscene.
  2. Kagetora — Port Bastok (F-6), inside the door to Warehouse 2.
  3. Ensetsu — back in Kaede's house, Port Bastok (I-5). The quest appears in your log after this scene.
  4. Korroloka Tunnel, map 5, (K-8). The quickest way in is from Western Altepa Desert. Examine the ??? to spawn 3 Korroloka Leeches. They link with nearby Thread Leeches, so clear the area first.
  5. When the leeches' bodies have despawned, examine the ??? again for the key item Strangely Shaped Coral.
  6. Ensetsu (Port Bastok) again.
  7. Ryoma — Norg (H-8). He gives you the Sealed Dagger.
  8. Ensetsu one last time. Ninja unlocked. You also get the title Shadow Walker.

PRO TIP — doing it with friends. One player spawns the leeches. After they despawn, the others should examine the ??? for their coral before the player who spawned them. If that player dies, don't raise them until the leeches are dead, or the leeches respawn.

CHAPTER 5

SHADOWS, DECODED

Here's everything Phoenix's code does with Utsusemi, so you can stop guessing.

The rules of shadows on Phoenix

Rule What the code does
How many Ichi: 3. Ni: 4 when NIN is your main job, 3 when it's your subjob.
How long 15 minutes, or until they're all gone.
What they block Each normal melee hit or single-target physical move eats one shadow and does nothing. Most single-target magical TP moves also eat one. Utsusemi shadows always block. Blink only blocks 80% of the time.
Ichi over Ichi Works any time. Ichi resets you to 3 shadows.
Ni over anything Works any time. Resets you to 4.
Ichi over Ni Fails ("no effect") as long as even one Ni shadow is left.
Enmity Casting Utsusemi: 1 CE / 300 VE. Every shadow a mob hits takes 25 CE off your hate with that mob.
Third Eye / Blink Utsusemi removes Third Eye. If you somehow have Blink too, Utsusemi shadows are used first.

FROM THE CODE — the overwrite rule. In enhancing_ninjutsu.lua each cast stores a "tier": Ichi is 1, Ni is 2. A new cast only lands if the shadows you have now are the same tier or lower. That's why Ichi can't overwrite Ni shadows, and why Ichi over Ichi always works. The shadow count itself (3 or 4) is stored separately, and Phoenix's override adds UTSUSEMI_BONUS gear on top of it. No era gear has that stat.

FROM THE CODE — shadows cost you hate. battleutils.cpp and mobskills.lua both take 25 CE per absorbed shadow off your enmity with that mob, citing the July 2005 update notes. A tank that only blocks with shadows slowly loses hate. Keep Provoking and keep casting.

The tank's shadow cycle

  1. Before the pull: Ni (4 shadows).
  2. Shadows down to 1–2: recast Ni if it's ready (45 s recast). It overwrites everything.
  3. Ni on cooldown and shadows at 0: cast Ichi (30 s recast). It lands because there are no Ni shadows left.
  4. Ni on cooldown but Ni shadows still up: wait. Ichi will fail. Use the time to Provoke, nuke or debuff.
  5. Repeat. With good timing you're almost never at zero.

WARNING — the 1-shadow trap. With 1 Ni shadow left and Ni on cooldown, you can't refresh. Every tank on Phoenix eats this sooner or later. Two ways to avoid it: recast Ni early (at 2 shadows), or time Ichi so its 4-second cast finishes after the last Ni shadow is gone. The overwrite check happens when the cast finishes, so if that last shadow is still up at that moment, Ichi does nothing and you've wasted the cast.

What goes straight through

Shadows only stop hits that target you with a physical or single-target attack. In Phoenix's code these ignore shadows: - Breath attacks (default for every breath move). - Status-only moves — sleeps, gazes, slows, poisons with no damage. - Moves explicitly marked "ignore shadows" — most AoE magic like Fireball, Sandspin and Bomb Toss.

And these wipe every shadow at once: moves marked "wipe shadows" (Tremors, Acid Mist, Wind Shear, Ill Wind, Stormwind and others). Chapter 6 has the list for every camp.

Multi-hit moves: a 3-hit move with 3+ shadows up is absorbed entirely and takes 3 shadows. With fewer shadows than hits, each hit takes one and the rest land.

FROM THE CODE — a modern bonus you get anyway. combat_utils.lua gives any NIN main or sub a 50% chance per shadow to keep it when an AoE physical move would take it. This mechanic comes from a much later update (Yagyu Darkblade era), but Phoenix's code doesn't switch it off. Enjoy it, but don't plan around it.

Elemental ninjutsu damage

Phoenix's code works out a nuke's damage in this order:

  1. Base: Ichi 16, Ni 69, San 134.
  2. Plus your INT minus the mob's INT (dINT): ×1 for Ichi and Ni, ×2 for San, up to 25 / 113 / 118 points of dINT. Past that, each point counts half, and nothing counts past three times that number. If dINT is negative, it subtracts 1 for 1.
  3. × your Ninjutsu skill bonus (main-job NIN only): 1 + (skill − floor) ÷ 200, using floors of 50 / 125 / 275 and caps of 250 / 350 / 500 for Ichi / Ni / San.
  4. × Magic Attack Bonus vs. the mob's Magic Defense, then resistance, weather and day.

CHAPTER 10

THE ENDGAME NINJA

At 75, Ninja has three jobs: tank what a Paladin shouldn't, deal damage, and pull.

Where Ninja shines on Phoenix

  • Evasion tank for mobs whose damage is mostly melee: shadows block what they do best. Check the mob's TP moves first. Breath and AoE heavy mobs go through shadows and belong to a Paladin.
  • Pulling with Tonko and shuriken. No Sneak of your own, so carry Silent Oil.
  • Damage with Kikoku, Fudo or Hayabusa and Blade: Jin / Ten / Ku.
  • Dynamis — the whole Koga set and Kikoku come from here.
  • Sky gods and ToAU NMs — Byakko's Haidate, Optical Hat.

The rules of endgame tanking

  1. Check the move list before you volunteer to tank. Chapter 6 shows how; the same logic applies to any NM.
  2. Enmity first. Provoke on cooldown, a nuke or Hojo between Provokes, and Enmity+ gear in your evasion set (Toreador's Cape, Storm Manopolas). Remember: every absorbed shadow costs 25 CE.
  3. Recast Ni at 2 shadows, not 0.
  4. Bring tools for twice as long as you expect.

Mijin Gakure: your last word

  • Deals 80% of your current HP as damage (resistable), then you die. At 75 with 1,100 HP, that's up to about 880 before resistance.
  • You don't lose EXP, but you come back with Weakness and normal Raise HP on Phoenix.
  • Use it to finish a mob that's about to wipe the party, while your HP is still high. At low HP it does almost nothing.

SHINOBI'S CHALLENGE

  • Tank Crawlers' Nest from 37 to 48 without casting a single cure on yourself.
  • Get the full Ninja AF set and Blade: Ku before 75.
  • Collect Fudo, Hayabusa +1 and Unji, and test which pair you like best.
