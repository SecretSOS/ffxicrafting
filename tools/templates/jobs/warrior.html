HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most Warrior guides on the web describe the game after 2009: Retaliation, Restraint, Blood Rage, Ukko's Fury, King's Justice and level-99 gear. None of that is on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with its era-revert modules applied. Ability values, weapon skill numbers, skill caps, gear stats and drops all come from that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki and HorizonXI Wiki, because the server code can't tell you where to stand or what an NPC says.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves time, TP or gil
  • WARNING — a common way to die or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • WARLORD'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — three things this guide can't settle from the code.

  • Warrior's Charge. In the code it lasts 60 seconds and only ends early when you use a weapon skill. Old-era guides describe it as "your next attack round". Test it once before you build a plan around it (Chapter 9).
  • Damage-type numbers (Chapter 5) come from the server's mob data files. They match the classic weaknesses (birds and bats take more from piercing, skeletons from blunt), but nobody has checked every value against 2007.
  • Live server settings such as weapon-skill points per kill aren't public. The numbers here are the code defaults.

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE WARRIOR

Other jobs have tricks. You have a bigger axe.

On Phoenix a WAR does four things, in this order of importance:

  1. Deal damage. Two-handed weapons, Double Attack, Berserk and the best weapon skill list in the game.
  2. Open and close skillchains. You'll have more weapon skills to choose from than anyone in the party.
  3. Hold hate when it goes wrong. Provoke on a 30-second timer, Defender, and enough HP to survive a few hits.
  4. Make everyone else stronger. Warcry raises the whole party's attack. /WAR is the most common subjob in the game.

FROM THE CODE — the era Berserk. Modern Berserk scales with level and lasts longer. Phoenix's era module (modules/era/lua/globals/job_utils/warrior.lua) puts it back to a flat +25% attack / −25% defense for 180 seconds at every level, and does the same for Defender. Warcry's power is floor(level ÷ 4 + 4.75) ÷ 256. That's 8.98% at 75 and 5.08% at 35, and the server stores buff power as a whole number, so you get +8% at 75 and +5% at 35.

FROM THE CODE — Warrior's Charge on Phoenix. The era module removes the Triple Attack part and sets Double Attack to 100%. The effect has a 60-second timer, and the only thing in the code that removes it early is using a weapon skill (scripts/globals/weaponskills.lua). So in the code it means "every swing double-attacks until you weapon skill or 60 seconds pass". If it ends after one swing in-game, the server has changed it.

No quest. No key item. Just pick up the axe.

Warrior is a starting job. You can pick it at character creation, or change to it at your Mog House at any time.

Berserk and Aggressor are both 3 minutes on a 5-minute recast, so you have both up 60% of the time. Line them up with your weapon skills, not with the pull.

PRO TIP — Warcry is a party skill. It's 30 seconds of extra attack for everyone in range. Use it right before your skillchain partner's weapon skill, not right after your own.

Double Attack

Double Attack 10% at 25, +1% per merit (up to 15%), plus gear: Fighter's Calligae +1%, Pole Grip +2%, Brutal Earring +5%. A hit that double-attacks gives TP for both swings, so Double Attack is TP gain as well as damage.

Damage types: why your axe sometimes hits like a wet noodle

Every weapon does one damage type: slashing (Great Axe, Axe, Great Sword, Scythe, Sword), piercing (Polearm, Dagger, Archery), blunt (Club, Staff) or hand-to-hand. Some mob families take more or less damage from each type.

FROM THE CODE — how Phoenix does it. Each mob family has resistance values in data/ecosystems.yaml, in units of 1/10,000. A value of −5,000 means your damage is multiplied by 0.5. A value of +2,500 means ×1.25. Most families have no values at all and take normal damage from every type. That's why the table below is mostly "neutral".

WARNING — troll, lamia and Mamool Ja don't resist slashing here. You'll see "slashing −50%" on those families in modern guides. In Phoenix's data only one named species in each family has it (for example the "general" troll species). The Hilltroll Warriors and Paladins in Mount Zhayolm take normal slashing damage.

FROM THE CODE — Tomahawk. Your merit Tomahawk doesn't add a flat weakness. It removes 25% of whatever value the mob already has, toward zero (scripts/effects/tomahawk.lua). On a skeleton (piercing ×0.5) it helps a lot. On a neutral mob it does nothing. On a bird it actually reduces the piercing weakness. Use it on resistant targets only.

Holding hate (and letting go of it)

  • Provoke adds 1,800 volatile enmity. Volatile enmity fades over time, so Provoke wins hate for a few seconds, not for the fight.
  • Fighter's gear has Enmity+. Fighter's Lorica +8, Mufflers +3, Cuisses +2, Calligae +1, Mask +1. Each point of Enmity in gear adds 1% to the enmity you generate (clamped at −50% to +100% on Phoenix).
  • Defender is for holding hate long enough for the real tank to Flash or Provoke back. It's not a tanking stance.

WARNING — Berserk makes you squishy. −25% defense is a lot when you pull hate with a Raging Rush. If you're about to take hate, swap Berserk for Defender or let the tank get it back first.

CHAPTER RECAP

  • Berserk + Aggressor + Warcry, timed with your weapon skill.
  • Most leveling mobs are neutral. Birds, bats and flies take +25% from piercing; skeletons take +25% from blunt.
  • Tomahawk only helps on mobs that resist your weapon.

Bracket by bracket

Levels 1–10 — home nation. Bronze/Brass Axe or Butterfly Axe. Kill everything that checks Easy Prey to Even Match outside your city, and weapon skill every time you reach 100% TP. No subjob yet, so fight and rest.

Levels 10–20 — Valkurm Dunes, Buburimu Peninsula, Korroloka. Parties start here. All three AF quests start in Bastok Mines and need Warrior as your main job. Steps were read from Phoenix's quest scripts and checked against BG Wiki and HorizonXI Wiki.

AF1 — The Doorman (Razor Axe, Lv40)

  1. Talk to Phara in Bastok Mines (J-9).
  2. Go to Davoi. Check the Hide Flap around (K-9/K-10). Two Orc NMs spawn: Gavotvut and Barakbok. Kill both, then check the flap again for the key item Sword Grip Material.
  3. Return to Phara. Wait one game day (zone out and back after midnight Vana'diel time), then talk to her again for Yasin's Sword.
  4. Take it to Naji in the Metalworks (J-8) for the Razor Axe.

WARNING — the flap moves. After someone checks it, the Hide Flap moves to another spot and the NMs have a 5-minute repop per person. If it isn't where you expect, walk the nearby tunnels.

  3. Go to Behemoth's Dominion and check the ??? at K-9. Three Goblins spawn: Doglix Muttsnout (WHM), Moxnix Nightgoggle (RNG) and Picklix Longindex (THF). Kill all three.
