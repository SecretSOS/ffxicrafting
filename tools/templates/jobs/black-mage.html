HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most Black Mage guides on the web describe the game after 2009: Elemental Celerity, Magic Burst Bonus traits, -ja spells, Manawell, Cascade, Subtle Sorcery, Death, Scholar subjob and 99-era Ancient Magic. None of that is on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with its era-revert modules applied. Spell levels, the damage formula, trait values, skill caps, gear stats and drops all come from that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki, because the server code can't tell you where to stand or what an NPC says.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves time, MP or gil
  • WARNING — a common way to die or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • ARCHMAGE'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — three things this guide can't settle from the code.

  • Nuke damage uses LandSandBoat's modern player formula (base damage plus INT-difference brackets), because Phoenix's USE_OLD_MAGIC_DAMAGE setting is off by default. Phoenix does revert spell MP costs and casting times, and restores the old Magic Burst multiplier. Numbers in Chapter 5 are from that formula.
  • Ancient Magic II was reworked by Phoenix so rank 1 only unlocks each spell. Extra ranks add magic accuracy and Magic Burst damage.
  • Live server settings such as resting MP and enmity caps aren't public. The numbers here are the code defaults.

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE BLACK MAGE

Pointy hat, big numbers, short life expectancy.

On Phoenix a BLM does four things, in this order of importance:

  1. Nuke. Tier III and IV elemental magic, and Ancient Magic at 50+.
  2. Magic Burst. Nuke into a skillchain for ×1.30 or more.
  3. Sleep. Sleep and Sleepga control links and adds.
  4. Stun. Stun at 45 cancels a mob's TP move or spell.

Job abilities

Lv Ability Recast What it does (Phoenix code)
1 Manafont 2 hours Spells cost no MP for 60 seconds
15 Elemental Seal 10:00 Your next elemental or enfeebling spell gets +256 magic accuracy (lasts 60 s)

FROM THE CODE — Elemental Seal is accuracy, not damage. It adds 256 magic accuracy (scripts/combat/basic/magic_hit_rate.lua), so the next spell almost can't be resisted. It doesn't work on Dark or Divine magic. Use it on Sleepga when an add has to stay down, or on a big Ancient Magic nuke.

FROM THE CODE — what Conserve MP really saves. When it triggers (25% of casts), the spell costs a random 8/16 to 16/16 of its MP (src/map/ai/states/magic_state.cpp). That's about 9% of your MP saved on average, not 25%.

CHAPTER 5

BLACK MAGE MATH

How a nuke turns into a number.

The nuke formula (Phoenix, player version)

FROM THE CODE — damage in six steps (scripts/globals/spells/damage_spell.lua). 1. Base damage = the spell's base, plus a bonus for every point of INT you have over the mob (the bonus per point drops as the gap grows). 2. × Magic Attack Bonus: (100 + your MAB) ÷ (100 + the mob's Magic Defense). 3. × Elemental staff: +10% (NQ) or +15% (HQ) for the matching staff. 4. × Day and weather: +10% on the matching day, +10% single weather, +25% double weather. These only apply on a third of casts unless you wear the matching elemental obi. 5. × Magic Burst, if the nuke lands in a skillchain's window. 6. × Resist: full, half, quarter or eighth damage.

Base damage by INT difference (before step 2):

Spell MP dINT 0 +25 +50 +75 +100 +150
Blizzard III 120 320 390 460 525 590 680
Blizzaga II 175 370 440 510 575 640 730
Blizzard IV 164 560 657 755 845 935 1075
Freeze 307 700 750 800 850 900 1000
Freeze II (merit) 287 800 850 900 950 1000 1100

PRO TIP — tier IV beats Ancient Magic per MP. Blizzard IV does 80% of Freeze's base damage at even INT, and matches or beats it once you're about 75 INT over the mob, for about half the MP. Save Ancient Magic for Magic Bursts and kills that must happen now.

WARNING — INT below the mob's doesn't cost you on Phoenix's formula, but INT above it pays. In the player formula a negative INT gap adds no bonus but takes nothing away. Every point of INT gear above the mob's INT adds damage until the bracket rates fall off.

Magic Burst

FROM THE CODE — Phoenix's Magic Burst. Phoenix restores the pre-2015 multiplier: ×1.25 + 0.05 per skillchain step: ×1.30 on the first skillchain, +5% each time the chain continues, capped at ×1.50 (modules/era/lua/combat/magic_burst.lua). Your nuke must match one of the skillchain's elements and land before the window closes.

Skillchain Burst with
Liquefaction / Fusion / Light Fire (Light also takes Thunder, Wind, Light)
Induration / Distortion Blizzard
Detonation / Fragmentation Aero (Fragmentation also takes Thunder)
Scission / Gravitation Stone (Gravitation also takes Dark)
Impaction Thunder
Reverberation / Distortion Water
Darkness Blizzard, Stone or Water

Elemental staves (your best purchase)

Every staff adds +10 magic accuracy and +5% damage per point of its element bonus. NQ staves are +2 (+20 accuracy, +10% damage); HQ staves are +3 (+30 accuracy, +15% damage). Ice and Thunder are the ones you'll use most; Dark (Pluto's Staff) is for Sleep, Sleepga, Drain, Aspir and Stun.

MONEY MAKER — Woodworking 75. Every NQ staff is Ebony Lumber + that element's bead; the HQ is the HQ result.

Hate, MP and survival

  • Ancient Magic and tier IV pull hate. Let the tank build enmity first; nuke after the second weapon skill.
  • Resting: Clear Mind gives +15 MP per tick at 75 on top of the base.
  • Aspir at 25 drains MP from mobs with MP (Chapter 6's report says which camps have them). Pluto's Staff helps it land.

CHAPTER RECAP

  • Base damage × MAB ratio × staff × day/weather × burst × resist.
  • Magic Burst ×1.30 on the first skillchain, up to ×1.50.
  • Tier IV for MP efficiency; Ancient Magic for bursts.

PRO TIP — what the report tells you. Blizzard is the default nuke for most of the climb: lizards, beetles, crabs, flies and birds all take it. Worms and Antica want Aero; Hilltrolls want Water. Garlaige bats don't sleep, so Bind (ice) holds them instead.

Bracket by bracket

Levels 1–10 — home nation. Melee with a staff, Stone the pull, rest a lot. Bind (7) on anything that runs at you.

Levels 10–20 — Valkurm Dunes, Buburimu Peninsula, Korroloka. MAB +20 at 10. Fire at 13, Blizzard at 17, Sleep at 20. In parties, you're nuking after the second weapon skill and sleeping links.

Levels 18–20 — subjob. Do The Old Lady or Elder Memories. Level RDM to 15 for Fast Cast.

Levels 20–30 — Qufim, Yhoator and Yuhtunga jungles, Bibiki Bay. Conserve MP at 20. Aspir at 25 — drain every mob that has MP between nukes. Escape at 29. Warp at 17 gets you home for free.

PRO TIP — Race Specific Equipment (RSE) at level 25. Every race has its own four-piece set (Lv27–33) from The Goblin Tailor (Guttrix, Lower Jeuno). You can only get it during your race's 7 h 41 m "week", in one of three dungeons, with that dungeon's chest key — so farm the key before your week starts. For Black Mages: Hume Boots (INT+3, AGI+3), Elvaan Gauntlets (MP+24) and Ledelsens (MP+20, INT+2), Mithra Loincloth (MP+32), Galka Surcoat and Bracers (MP+32 each). Tarutaru pieces add HP, which a Taru BLM needs for survival, not damage. Full walkthrough: Race Specific Equipment guide. Live race and dungeon schedule: Phoenix RSE calendar.

Levels 30–40 — Garlaige Citadel, Eastern Altepa, Eldieme Necropolis. MAB +24 at 30. Sleepga at 31 — the start of burst parties and AoE farming. Blizzaga at 32. Warp II at 40.

Levels 40–50 — Crawlers' Nest, Attohwa Chasm, Gustav Tunnel. AF1 at 40 for the Casting Wand (MP+20). Stun at 45: watch the mob's TP and Stun the dangerous move. Moldavite Earring (MAB+5, Mysticmaker Profblix in Labyrinth of Onzozo) at 47. Freeze at 50, MAB +28.

WARNING — Freeze pulls hate. A 300-MP Ancient Magic spell landing early takes the mob off the tank. Wait until the tank has a few weapon skills of hate, or burst it into a skillchain.

Levels 50–60 — Quicksand Caves, Kuftal Tunnel, ToAU jungles. AF2 and AF3. Elemental staves at 51. Shaman's Cloak (INT+4, MP+25, Centurio X-I in Quicksand Caves) at 56. Sleepga II at 56. Flare at 60.

Levels 60–70 — Kuftal Tunnel, Mamook, Caedarva Mire. Tier III nukes in your 60s. Black Cloak (Refresh+1, INT+4, Clothcraft 89; it blocks the head slot) at 68 for idling. MAB +32 at 70.

Levels 70–75 — Mount Zhayolm, Caedarva Mire, Lufaise Meadows. Errant / Mahatma pieces (big INT) at 72. Igqira Weskit (MAB+6, Elemental +5) at 73. Tier IV nukes from 73. Retribution quest at Staff 230 (level 73). Spend merits on Ancient Magic II.

CHECKPOINT — level 75

  • Elemental at 276, Dark at 269.
  • HQ Ice, Thunder and Dark staves at minimum.
  • Ancient Magic II unlocked (Chapter 8).

NOT ON PHOENIX (DON'T FARM THESE)

WARNING — these show up in "best BLM gear" lists, but you can't get them here.

  • Zeni Notorious Monster drops — the Oracle's set (Sarameya, Tinnin) and other ZNM loot. Phoenix hides the ZNM pop NPCs and spawn points (modules/phoenix/lua/zones/npc_visibility.lua).
  • Einherjar and Nyzul Isle rewards — both NPCs are hidden.
  • Goliard pieces — in the database, but no drop, quest or shop on Phoenix gives them.
  • Nashira pieces — Salvage gear. Phoenix has no Salvage; the only source in the code is a later (Rhapsodies) vendor.
  • Snow Ring / Dark Ring — Goldsmithing 102 recipes; the era craft cap is 100.

ARCHMAGE'S CHALLENGE — the Zenith Mitts. Fafnir and Genbu drop the Aquarian Abjuration: Hands. Craft the Cursed Mitts, trade both to Alphollon C Meriard in Northern San d'Oria, and you have MAB+5 in the hands slot.

CHAPTER 8

QUESTS, AF AND ENDGAME

The Wizard's set is a Windurst story. Bring a Tarutaru friend.

AF1 · The Three Magi (level 40) → Casting Wand

  1. Talk to Chumimi in Heavens Tower (Windurst Walls) as a level 40+ Black Mage. Pick a side (Koru-Moru, Shantotto or Yoran-Oran); it only changes the title you get.
  2. Trade any elemental crystal to a Telepoint (the crystal formations in Xarcabard, La Theine, Konschtat, Tahrongi, Altepa and Yhoator) to get a Faded Crystal.
  3. In Xarcabard, trade the Faded Crystal to the ??? to spawn the Chaos Elemental. Kill it; it always drops the Glowstone.
  4. Trade the Glowstone to Chumimi.

AF2 · Recollections (level 50) → Wizard's Sabots

  1. Zone out after AF1, then talk to Chumimi as a level 50+ Black Mage.
  2. Talk to Laityn in Ru'Lude Gardens.
  3. Trade a Bag of Seeds to Chumimi. Fire and Water Elementals in Crawlers' Nest drop it often.
  4. Get a Whine Cellar Key from a Demon Chancellor, Commander, General or Magistrate in Castle Zvahl Baileys (uncommon drop).
  5. Trade the key to the Ore Door in Castle Zvahl Keep for the Foe Finder Mk I.
  6. Return to Chumimi.

AF3 · The Root of the Problem (level 50) → Wizard's Petasos

  1. Talk to Chumimi after AF2 (zone out first).
  2. Trade a Square of Silk Cloth to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls.
  3. Talk to Chumimi for the Sluice Surveyor Mk I.
  4. In Toraimarai Canal, check two ??? markers, then the third to spawn Magic Sludge.
  5. Kill it and return to Chumimi. You get the Wizard's Petasos and the title Paragon of Black Mage Excellence.

WARNING — Magic Sludge is built to stop nukers. Phoenix gives it −75% magic damage taken, −85% physical damage taken, immunity to Sleep and Blind, and attacks at 150% strength (scripts/zones/Toraimarai_Canal/mobs/Magic_Sludge.lua). Bring a full party and plenty of MP.

Borghertz's Sorcerous Hands (level 50, after AF2) → Wizard's Gloves

  1. Talk to Guslam in Upper Jeuno as a level 50+ Black Mage who has finished Recollections.
  2. Open a Treasure Coffer in Garlaige Citadel for the Old Gauntlets.
  3. Report to Guslam. On your first Borghertz quest you then talk to Deadly Minnow (Upper Jeuno) and pay Yin Pocanakhu (Lower Jeuno) 1,000 gil; later Borghertz quests skip this.
  4. Check the ??? in Port Jeuno, then kill Dark Spark at the Torch in Castle Zvahl Baileys and check the Torch for Shadow Flames.
  5. Return to the ??? in Port Jeuno.

Coffers (only after you start Borghertz)

Piece Zone
Wizard's Coat Monastic Cavern coffer
Wizard's Tonban The Eldieme Necropolis coffer

FROM THE CODE — the coffers wait for Borghertz. The AF body and legs only come out of these coffers once your Borghertz quest is started or done, and only on BLM main (scripts/quests/jeuno/helpers.lua).

Retribution · Blood and Glory

  1. Talk to Shantotto in Windurst Walls with Staff skill 230+ (level 73). You get the Weapon Training Guide and a Pole of Trials.
  2. Earn 300 weapon skill points with it (5 per WS, +2/+4/+6 for skillchain levels 1/2/3).
  3. Trade the Pole back to Shantotto for the Map to the Annals of Truth.
  4. In Ifrit's Cauldron, check the ??? to spawn Cailleach Bheur. Kill it, then check the ??? for the Annals of Truth.
  5. Return to Shantotto.

Black Halo · Orastery Woes

Same pattern with a Club of Trials and Club skill 230 (level 75): start with Kuroido-Moido in Port Windurst, and the NM is Eldhrimnir in Ro'Maeve.

Merits at 75

Group Merit Per rank (Phoenix)
Group 1 Fire / Ice / Wind / Earth / Thunder / Water magic potency +2 Magic Attack Bonus for that element
Group 1 Elemental Seal recast −20 s
Group 2 Freeze II, Flare II, Burst II, Quake II, Tornado II, Flood II Rank 1 unlocks; each extra rank +5 magic accuracy and +3% Magic Burst damage

FROM THE CODE — Phoenix's Ancient Magic II. Phoenix removes the post-2019 accuracy bonus from AM II and moves it into the merits: rank 1 only unlocks the spell, each extra rank adds +5 magic accuracy and +3% Magic Burst damage (modules/phoenix/lua/globals/spells/damage_spell.lua). One AM II at full ranks beats three at rank 1.

PRO TIP — merit order. Group 1: Ice and Thunder potency first (your main nukes). Group 2: pick one AM II (Freeze II or Burst II) and take it as high as you can.

Endgame where Black Mages matter

  • Dynamis — Sorcerer's relic armor; Sleepga on statue pulls.
  • Sky / Sea — Magic Bursts on gods, Stun on their big moves.
  • HNMs — Abjurations for the Zenith pieces; Stun on King Behemoth's Meteor and Fafnir's breath.
