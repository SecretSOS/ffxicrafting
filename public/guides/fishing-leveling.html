Quick Start

  1. Buy a Bamboo Fishing Rod (535g, Bastok Mines) or check the AH.
  2. Pick your nation's starting route, or head to Bibiki Bay.
  3. Buy a Carbon Fishing Rod from AH before skill 8.
  4. Save for a Halcyon Rod. Buy it before skill 38.
  5. Follow the universal route to 85. Park on Black Ghost at 76.

Purchasing Priority

Spend your gil in this order. Everything else can wait.

  1. Carbon Fishing Rod — needed for Moorish Idol (rank 9). Buy from AH.
  2. Shrimp Lure (5,730g) — lure for Moorish Idol. Buy from Fishing Guild. This is your moneymaker.
  3. Fishing Gear Set — Fishermans Tunica, Fishermans Gloves, Fishermans Hose, Fishermans Boots. Each piece adds fishing skill. Buy from AH as you can afford them.
  4. Halcyon Rod — your only goal from Moorish Idol onward. Save every gil. Buy before skill 38.
  5. Moat Carp stockpile — start saving for Lu Shang's Fishing Rod (10,000 Moat Carp quest) and daily Fishing Guild GP turn-ins (30 GP per Moat Carp). Fish them with Little Worm whenever you pass through a Moat Carp zone.
Chapter 2

How Fishing Skill-ups Work

Skill-ups only happen on successful catches. Line snaps, rod breaks, and lost fish give zero skill-up chance. This means your effective skill-up rate depends on how often you actually land the fish.

The Math

  • Effective skill-up rate = base chance × land rate. Below skill 50, the base chance is 60%. At 50+, it drops to 25%.
  • A fish far above your skill has a high lose chance — you hook it but can't reel it in. No skill-up.
  • A fish close to your skill has near 100% land rate. Every cast is a real skill-up opportunity.
  • Pool rarity matters too. High-value fish often have low rarity (450/1000), meaning many casts produce nothing or hook junk fish. When you finally hook the right one, you want 100% land rate.

The 50 Wall

At skill 50, the base skill-up chance drops from 60% to 25%. Everything slows down. This is where keeping 100% land rate matters most — you can't afford to lose catches when every successful one is your only shot at a skill-up.

The route below picks fish close to your level for fast skill-ups. Where a higher-value alternative exists, it's noted — you can always trade speed for money.

Chapter 5

After 85

At skill 85 you can continue to 100+ with an endgame rod, or pivot to money fishing. The Halcyon Rod carried you this far — now it's time to upgrade.

Endgame Rods

Lu Shang's Fishing Rod

  • Source: Turn in 10,000 Moat Carp / Forest Carp via the quest in Port San d'Oria.
  • Max Rank: 28 — up from Halcyon's 18. Handles every small fish comfortably.
  • Legendary: +1 effective durability vs snap/break, bonus attack and regen when fighting legendary fish.
  • Still small rod — large fish (e.g. Gugrusaurus) still need a large rod like Composite or Mithran.

Ebisu Fishing Rod

  • Source: Extremely rare. BCNM or quest reward (varies by server).
  • Max Rank: 30 — highest of any small rod in the game.
  • Legendary: Same bonuses as Lu Shang's, but with much higher attack bonus (50 vs 20).
  • Reality: Most players never see one. Lu Shang's is the practical endgame goal.

Money Fishing at 85+

  • Black Ghost (600g vendor, 100% land at 85+) — pure profit with zero risk.
  • Bastore Bream (600g vendor) — needs Lu Shang's Rod (rank 22 ≤ max rank 28) for 0% snap. On Halcyon it's 34% snap.
  • At 86+, Mercanbaligi and Ahtapot open up in Nashmau for continued progression.
