HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most Monk guides on the web describe the game after 2008: Footwork, Impetus, Perfect Counter, Ascetic's Fury, Victory Smite and level-99 gear. None of that is on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with its era-revert modules applied. Ability values, weapon skill numbers, skill caps, gear stats and drops all come from that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki and HorizonXI Wiki, because the server code can't tell you where to stand or what an NPC says.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves time, TP or gil
  • WARNING — a common way to die or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • GRANDMASTER'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — three things this guide can't settle from the code.

  • Counterstance's defense penalty depends on a server setting. With the default, Counterstance halves your defense; with the "old" setting, defense becomes 1 + VIT ÷ 2. Phoenix's live value isn't public.
  • Hand-to-hand damage types (Chapter 5) come from the server's mob data files, which give hand-to-hand its own column. Most families leave that column blank, so in Phoenix's data you hit flans and skeletons for normal damage. If the live server behaves differently, the data was changed.
  • Live server settings such as weapon-skill points per kill aren't public. The numbers here are the code defaults.

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE MONK

Two fists, two hits per round, and a kick when nobody's looking.

On Phoenix a MNK does four things, in this order of importance:

  1. Punch fast. Martial Arts cuts your attack delay every 15 levels. You swing twice per round with one "weapon".
  2. Build TP for the party. Fast hits mean fast TP and more weapon skills per fight than anyone else.
  3. Take a hit and heal yourself. Max HP Boost, Counter and Chakra make you the sturdiest damage dealer.
  4. Hold hate in an emergency. Not a tank, but a Monk with Counterstance can stand under a mob for a while.

FROM THE CODE — the era Focus, Dodge and Chakra. Phoenix's era module (modules/era/lua/globals/job_utils/monk.lua) reverts Focus to a flat Accuracy +20 and Dodge to a flat Evasion +20, both for 120 seconds (modern versions last 30 seconds and add crit or evasion bonuses). Chakra heals exactly VIT × 2, multiplied by any Chakra gear (Temple Cyclas +50%, Melee Gloves +30%). At 75 with about 75 VIT, that's 150 HP, or 225 in Temple Cyclas.

FROM THE CODE — Boost. Each use adds 12.5% attack (stored as a whole number, so +12%), lasts up to 3 minutes, and is used up at the end of your next attack round (battle_entity.cpp). Temple Gloves add +5.5% per use. Boost twice before a weapon skill if you have the TP sitting there.

What's NOT on Phoenix

WARNING — skip these sections of any modern guide. Phoenix runs pre-Wings of the Goddess.

  • Footwork, Impetus, Perfect Counter, Inner Strength — later expansions. The era module even has a commented-out Footwork, but nothing turns it on.
  • Ascetic's Fury, Victory Smite, Shijin Spiral — merit and later weapon skills. The weapon-skill merit category has 0 upgrades.
  • Tactical Guard, Tactical Parry — later traits.
  • ZNM gear (Enkidu's set, Hachiryu, Shuhansadyu Baghnakhs) — Phoenix hides the ZNM pop NPCs and spawn points.
  • Einherjar gear (Iota Ring) — the Einherjar NPC is hidden.
  • Hasso from /SAM — hand-to-hand isn't a two-handed weapon in Phoenix's code, so Hasso and Seigan won't activate.

CHAPTER 2

BECOMING A MONK

Before you can punch dragons, you have to punch a ghost in a mine.

Monk is a starting job. You can pick it at character creation or change to it at your Mog House.

Martial Arts: why your fists are fast

Every hand-to-hand weapon has a delay that already includes the 480 base. Martial Arts subtracts a flat amount from it, and you swing twice per round.

Your level Martial Arts Kaiser Knuckles (566) per round
1 −80 486
16 −100 466
31 −120 446
46 −140 426
61 −160 406
75 −180 386
75 + Hundred Fists — about 97

FROM THE CODE — Hundred Fists. battle_entity.cpp takes your weapon delay minus Martial Arts, sets a floor of 1.6 seconds, then multiplies by 0.25. Haste doesn't stack on top of it. 45 seconds of that is roughly 28 attack rounds instead of 7.

PRO TIP — low-delay weapons are a Monk trick. Tropical Punches have 1 damage but a 480 delay. With Martial Arts you swing far faster than with Brass Knuckles (576). Faster swings mean faster TP. Swap to a high-damage weapon once your level-based base damage is doing most of the work.

Levels 1–15: before Counter and Dodge

You're one of the best soloers in the game at this level. Boost before every pull, fight Easy Prey to Decent Challenge, and rest. Counter at 10 does free damage when mobs hit you.

CHECKPOINT — level 18

  • Purple Belt quest done (if you have the Norg fame).
  • Subjob quest done (Chapter 3).
  • Tropical Punches +1 or a cheap upgrade in hand.

CHAPTER 5

MONK MATH (SPEED, KICKS, COUNTERS AND HATE)

A Monk who knows the numbers spends less time on the floor.

Your buffs

Buff Effect Lasts Recast
Boost Attack +12% per use, gone after your next round until you swing 0:15
Focus Accuracy +20 2:00 5:00
Dodge Evasion +20 2:00 5:00
Counterstance Counter +45%, defense down 5:00 5:00
/WAR Berserk Attack +25%, Defense −25% 3:00 5:00

Focus and Dodge last 2 minutes on a 5-minute timer, so they're up 40% of the time. Save Focus for the weapon skill window on high-evasion mobs.

Haste: where your belt comes in

Gear haste caps at 25% in Phoenix's delay code (battle_entity.cpp), separate from magic haste and job-ability haste. Monk has the best haste belts in the game:

Belt Lv Haste How
Purple Belt 18 +4% Silence of the Rams
Brown Belt 40 +8% Fistful of Fury
Black Belt 70 +12% (and STR+7, physical damage taken −5%) Beat Around the Bushin

Black Belt alone is half the gear cap. The belt quests are in Chapter 8.

Counter and Kick Attacks

  • Counter (10% at 10, +45% with Counterstance) hits back when a mob swings at you. A counter doesn't need TP or your attack round, so it's free damage.
  • Kick Attacks (10% at 51, 12% at 71) add a kick to your attack round. Melee Hose adds +5%, merits +1% each.

WARNING — Counterstance and defense. By default, Counterstance halves your defense (battle_entity.cpp). A server setting can switch it to "defense = 1 + VIT ÷ 2" instead, which is even worse with good armor. Use it when you're meant to be hit (solo, NM kiting the tank can't hold), not in a normal party.

Damage types: hand-to-hand is its own type here

FROM THE CODE — no fallback to blunt. When you punch, Phoenix multiplies damage only by the mob's hand-to-hand resistance (battleutils.cpp), never its blunt resistance. Most families in data/ecosystems.yaml list values for slashing, piercing and blunt but leave hand-to-hand blank. So in Phoenix's data, flans, puddings and most skeletons take normal damage from your fists, even though they resist or like blunt.

Families with a hand-to-hand value (from the server data):

Family Hand-to-hand What to do
Leech, hecteyes ×0.75 Still fine to punch
Ghost (one species) ×0.5 Formless Strikes, or let the mages work
Slime (slime, clot, scum) ×0.25 Formless Strikes
Elementals (all eight) ×0.25 Don't punch. Formless Strikes at 75
Skeleton ("skeleton" species), corse ×1.125 Punch away
Qutrub ×2.0 Free damage

PRO TIP — Formless Strikes is your answer to bad matchups. It turns your melee into non-physical damage, so a slime's 75% resistance doesn't apply. You lose 30–40% raw damage but skip the 75% cut.

Holding hate (and not)

  • Monks pull hate because they deal the most damage. Subtle Blow (+20 at 65, plus gear) slows the mob's TP, not your hate.
  • Melee Crown has Enmity −3. Arhat's gear and Black Belt reduce physical damage when you do take hate.
  • /NIN gives you shadows when you can't stop pulling hate.

CHAPTER RECAP

  • Black Belt is the single biggest Monk upgrade: +12% haste.
  • Counterstance halves your defense by default. Use it on purpose.
  • In Phoenix's data your fists ignore blunt resistances; only a few families resist hand-to-hand.

Bracket by bracket

Levels 1–10 — home nation. Cesti +1 and Boost before every pull. Combo is your weapon skill. Monk solos better than most jobs here.

Levels 10–20 — Valkurm Dunes, Buburimu Peninsula, Korroloka. Tropical Punches +1 at 10 (Accuracy +12, delay 480). Counter at 10, Dodge and Max HP Boost at 15. Parties will hand you the pull because you can take a hit. Purple Belt at 18 if you have Norg fame 2 (Chapter 8).

Levels 18–20 — subjob. Do The Old Lady or Elder Memories, then level WAR to 10 at least. /WAR gives Double Attack at sub 25 (your level 50), but Provoke and Berserk help right away.

Levels 20–30 — Qufim, Yhoator and Yuhtunga jungles, Bibiki Bay. Focus at 25. Lynx Baghnakhs (Lv24, San d'Oria quest The General's Secret, delay 510) or Republic Knuckles.

PRO TIP — Race Specific Equipment (RSE) at level 25. Every race has its own four-piece set (Lv27–33) from The Goblin Tailor (Guttrix, Lower Jeuno). You can only get it during your race's 7 h 41 m "week", in one of three dungeons, with that dungeon's chest key — so farm the key before your week starts. For Monks: Hume gloves (STR+3 DEX+3), the whole Tarutaru Wonder set (STR and HP), Mithra gaiters (STR+3) and Mithra gauntlets (VIT+4, more Chakra). Full walkthrough: Race Specific Equipment guide. Live race and dungeon schedule: Phoenix RSE calendar.

Levels 30–40 — Garlaige Citadel, Eastern Altepa, Eldieme Necropolis, Castle Oztroja. Raging Fists at skill 125 (about level 41). Chakra at 35. Castle Oztroja has two Yagudo NMs with Monk prizes. Mee Deggi the Punisher always drops one item from a group: usually Impact Knuckles (Lv32), about 1 time in 10 the Ochimusha Kote (Lv34, Attack +20). Quu Domi the Gallant drops Sarutobi Kyahan (Lv39, Haste +3%) about 1 time in 10. Peacock Amulet at 33. Brown Belt at 40 (Haste +8%).

GRANDMASTER'S CHALLENGE — the Oztroja run. Ochimusha Kote at 34 are a Monk's best hands until the high 60s. Get a group of 35s and farm the Yagudo NM pops in Castle Oztroja for both the Kote and the Kyahan.

Levels 40–50 — Crawlers' Nest, Attohwa Chasm, Gustav Tunnel. AF1 at 40 (Beat Cesti). Jujitsu Gi (Poisonhand Gnadgad, Davoi) or Fomor Tunic (Eba, Phomiuna Aqueducts) for the body. Patas +1 (Lv48, Smithing 51 HQ) is the big-damage weapon. Get Crawlers' Nest coffer keys for Borghertz's Striking Hands.

Levels 50–60 — Quicksand Caves, Kuftal Tunnel, Wajaom Woodlands, Bhaflau Thickets. AF2 and AF3. Cross-Counters (BCNM) or Retaliators (Western Shadow, Fei'Yin) add Counter +5. Scorpion Harness +1 at 57 (Accuracy +12). Kick Attacks at 51. Howling Fist at 60.

Levels 60–70 — Kuftal Tunnel, Mamook, Caedarva Mire. Dragon Kick at 65 (skill 225). Tabin Jupon +1 and Tabin Bracers +1, Amemet Mantle +1 or Behemoth Mantle +1. Feral Fangs (Lv66) or Dragon Claws +1 (Lv68). Avengers (Bomb Queen) if your linkshell farms Ifrit's Cauldron.

Levels 70–75 — Mount Zhayolm, Caedarva Mire, Lufaise Meadows. Black Belt the moment you can (Chapter 8). Hand-to-Hand 250 at 70 for the Asuran Fists quest. Kaiser Knuckles at 75. Rasetsu Tekko +1 and Sune-ate +1 are the counter-tank pieces.

CHECKPOINT — level 75

  • Hand-to-Hand at 276 (cap).
  • Black Belt or Brown Belt on.
  • Asuran Fists quest started (Chapter 8).

FROM THE CODE — Formless Strikes. battleutils.cpp starts at 55% and adds 5% per merit, so 1 rank is 60% and 3 ranks are 70%. The damage is treated as non-physical, so physical damage reduction and damage-type resistances don't apply.

Other merits worth taking

  • STR, DEX or VIT (attributes) — VIT feeds Chakra and two of your weapon skills.
  • Hand-to-Hand skill — skill over cap is accuracy.
  • HP — more for Chakra to refill and more cushion for counter-tanking.

The rules of endgame damage
