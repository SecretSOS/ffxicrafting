HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most White Mage guides on the web were written for the game after 2008: Afflatus Solace, Esuna, Cura, Sacrifice, level 99 and gear with Cure Potency on every slot. None of that exists on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with the era-revert modules applied. Every spell level, MP cost, scroll price, gear stat and drop in here came out of that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki and FFXIclopedia, because the server code can't tell you what an NPC says or where to stand.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves you MP, time or a party
  • WARNING — a common way to wipe or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • HEALER'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — one thing this guide can't settle from the code. Phoenix's code ships with two single-target cure formulas: the modern one (2012 onward) and the era one. Which one the live server uses is a setting that isn't in the public repository. Chapter 5 gives both and a 30-second in-game test that tells you which one you're on.

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE WHITE MAGE

You won't top the damage meter or get many thank-yous. But the party only gets to keep pulling for as long as you can.

On Phoenix a WHM does four jobs, in this order of importance:

  1. Keep the tank alive with single-target cures sized to the damage, without pulling hate off them.
  2. Fix status ailments before they spread: Poisona, Paralyna, Silena, Stona, Erase.
  3. Raise the dead and keep yourself Reraised at endgame.
  4. Buff — Protectra, Shellra, Haste, Regen, Bar-spells — and move people with Teleports.

  • Your job is the tank's HP bar, then status, then raises, then buffs.

CHAPTER 2

BECOMING A WHITE MAGE

WHM is a starting job. You can pick it at character creation, or change to it at any Moogle.

Your first shopping list (levels 1–20)

You start with Cure. Buy the rest from the white-magic scroll vendors in the three nations: Regine (Port San d'Oria), Kususu (Port Windurst), Sororo (Bastok Markets). For the -ra Bar-spells, go to Hasim or Susu in Lower Jeuno.

FROM THE CODE — conquest-locked scrolls. The nation vendors use conquest standings. Some scrolls only show up when that nation is ranked 1st or 2nd that week, and a few (Slow, Diaga, Stoneskin) only for that nation's citizens while it's 1st. In the spell table in Chapter 4, ² means "nation 1st or 2nd" and ¹ means "your nation 1st". If a scroll is missing, check another nation's vendor or come back after the weekly tally.

Levels 1–11: solo first

There's no real party camp before 11 (see the project's leveling-camps guide). Solo on easy and decent-challenge mobs outside your starting city with a club, Dia and Banish. Cure yourself between fights and rest a lot.

PRO TIP — undead are WHM food. Cure spells damage undead instead of healing them (cure.lua calls the damage path on undead). Banish and Holy are Light-element. If your party is short a damage dealer around undead (Bones, Ghouls, Skeletons), your nukes actually matter there.

CHECKPOINT — level 11

  • Cure II, Protect, Poisona, Paralyna, Dia, Banish learned.
  • Signet up before every outing (bonus XP, faster resting).
  • About 1,000 gil left over for Shell and Curaga at 16–17.

CHAPTER 5

HEALING, DECODED

How much your spells heal, how much hate they cause, and which gear actually changes the numbers. All of it is read from Phoenix's code.

The two cure formulas

Single-target Cures Curagas
Modern formula (2012+, the code's default) Power = ⌊MND ÷ 2⌋ + ⌊VIT ÷ 4⌋ + Healing skill —
Era formula (if Phoenix turned it on) Power = 3 × MND + VIT + 3 × ⌊Healing skill ÷ 5⌋ Always this one, whatever the setting

Power goes into a table of breakpoints for each spell, and the result is never lower than the spell's floor. After that, the game multiplies by Cure Potency (capped at 50%), then by the day/weather bonus, then by ×2 for Divine Seal (globals/magic.lua).

FROM THE CODE — what each formula rewards. Under the modern formula, one point of Healing skill is worth two points of MND, and at 75 the breakpoints divide power down so much that MND gear barely moves single cures. Under the era formula, one point of MND is worth five points of skill. Curagas always use the era formula, so MND always matters for Curagas.

Cure enmity — what pulls hate onto you

FROM THE CODE — cure hate. Every HP you restore adds hate with every monster that's fighting the person you healed. That's 40 Cumulative + 240 Volatile per HP, divided by a number that grows with the healed player's level: 20 at level 10, 40 at 50, 55 at 75. (enmity_container.cpp, battleutils.cpp.)

  • It counts only the HP actually restored. A Cure IV on someone missing 50 HP costs a Cure IV's MP but only causes 50 HP of hate.
  • At 75, a 470-HP Cure IV on the tank adds about 340 CE + 2,050 VE to you.
  • Enmity− gear lowers all of it, capped at −50% (CalculateEnmityBonus).

So the classic rule holds on Phoenix: size the Cure to the damage. Cure III (46 MP) for chip damage, Cure IV for a weapon skill, Cure V when the tank is in trouble. Overcuring doesn't cause extra hate, but it wastes MP.

CHAPTER 10

THE ENDGAME HEALER

Endgame on Phoenix is Dynamis, Limbus, Sky, Sea, HNMs, Assault, Besieged, Salvage and Nyzul Isle. The WHM's job is the same in all of them: keep the tank up, keep yourself alive to raise, and keep Reraise on.

The rules of endgame healing

  1. Reraise on entry, every time. It lasts an hour (Chapter 4). A dead WHM with Reraise is back in 30 seconds. A dead WHM without it is a wipe.
  2. Stay out of AoE range and in cure range. Cures reach 20 yalms (spell_range 200 in the spell table). Many AoE TP moves cover about 10 yalms around the mob (Geist Wall's script says 10). Stand between the two.
  3. Hate is your biggest danger. Cure enmity piles up on every mob fighting the person you heal (Chapter 5). In multi-mob fights (Dynamis pulls, Besieged) that adds up fast. Wear full Enmity− and let the tank Provoke.
  4. Divine Seal + Curaga III/IV is your party-wide panic button. Benediction is the bigger one, every 2 hours.
  5. Devotion your BLMs between waves. Martyr only works within 6 yalms, so it's a melee-range trick for emergencies, not routine.

PRO TIP — Raise III brings people back with 50% HP and 90% of lost XP (Chapter 4). It's 250 MP, so in a long fight, Raise II plus a Cure IV is usually the better MP trade.

MONEY MAKER — what a WHM sells. Teleports (Chapter 4), Raises outside events, and party spots: on a 75-era server a WHM can get into almost any XP or merit party. Crafting gear for other mages (Raven/Crow sets, Noble's Tunic, Errant/Mahatma) pairs well with WHM if you level Clothcraft.
