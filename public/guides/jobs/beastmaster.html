CHAPTER 1

MEET THE BEASTMASTER

You don't tank. You don't nuke. You walk up to the scariest thing in the zone, smile, and make it your friend.

BST plays two ways:

  1. Charm. Take a wild mob and use it to kill other mobs. Strongest pets, free, and the core of the job from 1–75.
  2. Call Beast (jug pets). Equip a jug in the ammo slot and summon a familiar. Weaker than a good charm, but it never breaks free and never turns on you.

The rhythm of BST soloing: Charm a strong mob → Fight a weak mob → Reward/Heel as needed → kill → repeat → when charm runs low, Leave, then re-charm or kill the pet yourself.

CHAPTER 2

BECOMING A BEASTMASTER

Three quests, all in Jeuno. You need a level 30 job.

Step 1: Chocobo's Wounds (Upper Jeuno)

  1. Find the injured Chocobo in Upper Jeuno, next to Osker (by the chocobo stables).
  2. Trade it Gausebit Wildgrass, one clump at a time. Each feeding has a ~45 second cooldown, and it takes several feedings. Bring a stack.
  3. Keep feeding and talking until the quest completes.

Step 2: Save My Son (Lower Jeuno → Qufim Island)

  1. At level 30+, check the Merchant's House door in Lower Jeuno (!pos -82 -7 -168). Accept the quest.
  2. Talk to Shalott in Upper Jeuno.
  3. Go to Qufim Island and examine the Nightflowers (!pos -264 -3 28). Only works from 21:30 to 05:40 Vana'diel time.
  4. Return to the Merchant's House door in Lower Jeuno.
  5. Reward: 2,100 gil + Beast Whistle (Neck, Lv24 BST: VIT+2 CHR+2). That's your first charm piece.

Step 3: Path of the Beastmaster (Upper Jeuno)

  1. Talk to Brutus in Upper Jeuno (!pos -55 8 95).
  2. BST is unlocked. Change jobs at your Mog House.

PRO TIP — Qufim at night is aggro-heavy for a level 30. Bring Sneak/Invisible or Silent Oils/Prism Powders.

CHECKPOINT — BST unlocked, Beast Whistle in your bag.

WARNING — matchups that work against you. These are pairings older guides recommend. On Phoenix the target is the predator, so your pet's swings fail 5% of the time:

  • Moss Eaters vs. Boyahda's Death Caps or Mourioche. Turn it around: charm the plant and kill the rabbit.
  • Crabs or Makara vs. Diatryma in Misareaux. Birds beat Aquans.
  • Tiger Familiar vs. Mandragoras. Plantoids beat Beasts.
  • Any Amorph pet vs. Pugils or Crabs. Aquans beat Amorphs.

The golden rule

Charm a mob that checks Decent Challenge or easier to you, and use it to kill mobs that check Even Match to Tough. You get XP for what the pet kills. The pet should be strong, the target should be worth XP, and ideally the pet is the target's natural predator (the glowing-badge rows above).

1–10 · Sarutabaruta / Gustaberg

  • Sarutabaruta (W. or E.): Charm a Crawler (4–7) and kill Mandragoras (4–6): vermin beat plantoids. Or charm a Mandragora and kill Savanna Rarabs (2–6): plantoids beat beasts. From 7, Giant Bees (7–8) are the strongest pets in the zone.
  • Gustaberg (N. or S.): The cleanest food chain in the starter areas. Charm a Rock Lizard (4–8) and kill Maneating Hornets (3–6). Then charm an Ornery Sheep (5–8) and kill the Rock Lizards.

WARNING — Don't charm worms (Stone Eaters, Tunnel Worms). They won't follow you, and you're stuck until the charm wears off. And watch for the Bombs at 8–10 (Balloon, Shrapnel). They aggro, and they can't be charmed.

9–17 · Tahrongi Canyon

  • 9–12: Charm a Pygmaioi (8–11) (Tahrongi's Mandragoras) and kill Canyon Rarabs (7–11). Plants beat beasts, and the two spawn all over the canyon at the same levels. Rarabs stop giving decent XP around 12–13.
  • 10–13: Charm a Killer Bee (10–12) and kill Pygmaioi and Strolling Saplings (7–11). Vermin beat plants, so this is the same chain one step up.
  • 13–17: Charm a Wild Dhalmel (14–16) and kill Killer Bees, Canyon Crawlers (12–13) and Akbabas (11–13).
  • Goblins and Yagudo can't be charmed. A charm attempt on them just Binds them, which makes a good escape (more on that in Ch.5).

16–26 · Buburimu Peninsula

This is the best food-chain zone in the game. Four families sit side by side: - Sylvestre (15–19, plantoid) → kill Mighty Rarabs (15–18) - Carnivorous Crawler (20–23, vermin) → kill Sylvestre - Zu (20–24, bird) → kill Snippers (19–22) and Shoal Pugils (24–27) - Snipper (aquan) → kill Poison Leeches (21–24)

Bull Dhalmels (21–24) are strong pets with no edge here. At 23 you can pop a Crab Familiar (Fish Broth) for the leeches.

26–31 · Maze of Shakhrami / Buburimu coast

  • Buburimu coast (25–28): Charm Shoal Pugils (24–27) and kill Poison Leeches.

CHAPTER 5

CHARM, DECODED

This is where Phoenix's code beats every wiki. These are the formulas the server actually runs.

Charm success chance

FROM THE CODE — scripts/globals/job_utils/beastmaster.lua

  • Start at 50% minus the target's charm resistance.

  • If the mob is higher level than your BST level, subtract per level of difference:

    • Mob 1–50: −3% per level
    • Mob 51–70: −5% per level
    • Mob 71+: −10% per level

  • Multiply by the mob's light-resistance rank (light-weak mobs are easier, up to ×1.5).

  • Add Charm Chance gear (staves only: Light Staff +10, Apollo's Staff +15).
  • Add your CHR minus the mob's CHR.
  • Capped between 0% and 95%.

What that means: being 1–2 levels under a level-71+ mob costs you 10–20%. Against a level 40 mob it barely matters. CHR matters 1-for-1, which makes CHR gear meaningful for success rate.

PRO TIP — Gauge doesn't count staff bonuses (the code passes includeMods = false). If Gauge says "might be able to charm," your actual odds with a Light Staff are 10% higher.

Charm duration

FROM THE CODE

  • Base = 1.25 × your CHR + 150 seconds
  • × a level-difference multiplier
    • 5% per point of "Charm +" gear

Here's what level difference does, at CHR 60 (base 225 seconds):

You vs. Mob Multiplier Charm lasts
Mob 3 levels higher ×0.19 ~40 sec
Mob 1 level higher ×0.66 ~2.5 min
Same level ×1.00 ~3.7 min
You're 2 higher ×1.78 ~6.7 min
You're 4 higher ×2.59 ~9.7 min
You're 6 higher ×3.47 ~13 min
You're 9+ higher ×6.00 (cap) ~22.5 min

The big lesson: level difference matters far more than CHR. +10 CHR adds 12.5 seconds to the base. Being 2 levels higher instead of even almost doubles the charm. A weaker pet that holds beats a strong pet that breaks.

WARNING — Charming above your level is a coin flip and a 40-second leash. When it breaks you're fighting two mobs.

Goblins and uncharmables

Uncharmable mobs (Goblins, NMs, etc.) aren't wasted. A failed charm on an uncharmable mob Binds it instead (an ice-element resist check). Use that to escape.

Leave & re-charm

Leave (35) releases your pet. If it's still within its spawn area, you can charm it again right away. A pet that was resting stays resting when re-charmed quickly.

PRO TIP — A charm timer addon (for example PetMe) is allowed under Phoenix's Rule 6, since it only reads state. Auto-reacting to the pet's state is not allowed. Read Rule 6 in the overview doc before installing anything.

The food chain (pet intimidation)

Every monster family has a natural predator. Pick a pet that's the predator of whatever you're fighting, and never send a pet into its own predator.

The two circles and three pairs (an arrow means "has the edge over"):

  • Land circle: 🐗 Beasts → 🦎 Lizards → 🐛 Vermin → 🌿 Plantoids → back to 🐗 Beasts
  • Sea & sky circle: 🦀 Aquans → 🪱 Amorphs → 🐦 Birds → back to 🦀 Aquans
  • Pairs (each has the edge over the other): 💀 Undead ⇄ 💣 Arcana · 🐉 Dragons ⇄ 😈 Demons · Luminions ⇄ Luminians
  • Outside the chain: 🗡️ Beastmen, Elementals, Humanoids, Empty and Archaic machines. Nobody has the edge over them, and they have no edge over anybody.

FROM THE CODE — what "having the edge" actually does on Phoenix. battleutils.cpp (addEcosystemKillerEffects, IsIntimidated)

  • Every mob, every charmed mob and every jug pet carries a 5% Killer effect against its prey family.
  • When a prey-family mob tries to melee, cast a spell or use an item on the predator, there's that much chance it's intimidated, and the action simply fails.
  • It's purely defensive. There's no damage bonus. Your pet doesn't hit harder; its prey just misses turns.
  • TP moves aren't checked. A mob's special attacks (and your pet's Sic moves) go off no matter what.
  • It works both ways in the same fight. Send a Tiger (Beast) at a Mandragora (Plantoid) and the tiger's swings fail 5% of the time.

SET 1b · FAMILIAR GRAB (the big pull)

Use this when you want to charm something well above your level and hold it with Familiar (2-hour) for a hard fight or a tough camp.

FROM THE CODE — why duration gear doesn't matter here

  • A mob 4+ levels above you gives a base charm of only ~10–20 seconds (at CHR 60).
  • Charm+1 (De Saintre's Axe) adds 5% of that: under 1 second.
  • Familiar adds a flat 25–30 minutes to a charmed pet, plus +10% max HP. It ignores how long the original charm was.
  • So the only thing that matters is landing the charm. Then hit Familiar right away.

CHAPTER 8

LEGENDARY QUESTS (ARTIFACT ARMOR)

AF1 — Wings of Gold (Lv40) → Barbaroi Axe

  1. Talk to Brutus (Upper Jeuno) as a level 40+ BST main.
  2. Open a Treasure Chest in Middle or Upper Delkfutt's Tower with a Delkfutt Chest Key (or Thief's Tools) to get the Guiding Bell key item.
  3. Return to Brutus.

AF2 — Scattered into Shadow (Lv50) → Beast Gaiters

  1. Talk to Brutus at 50+. You receive three Aquaflora key items.
  2. In Fei'Yin, examine the Underground Pool spots. One spawns Dabotz's Ghost. Kill it.
  3. Finish the pool steps (each Aquaflora is used at a pool).
  4. In Castle Oztroja, open a Treasure Chest (Oztroja key) to get the Beast Collar.
  5. Trade the Beast Collar to Tebhi in Castle Oztroja.
  6. Return to Brutus.

AF3 — A New Dawn (Lv50, after AF2) → Beast Trousers

  1. Check the Merchant's House door in Lower Jeuno as a level 50+ BST.
  2. Talk to Osker (Upper Jeuno) and trade him 1 Mahogany Lumber to get the Tamer's Whistle key item.
  3. In The Eldieme Necropolis, examine the Sarcophagus. It pops Sturm, Taifun and Trombe. Kill all three.
  4. Examine the Sarcophagus again for Beast Trousers.

WARNING — The code buffs the AF3 trio hard: 250% base damage, high Store TP, and sleep immunity. Bring a party. Don't solo this at 50.

AF Hands — Borghertz's Wild Hands → Beast Gloves

  1. Talk to Guslam (Upper Jeuno). On your first Borghertz quest you'll also need to talk to Deadly Minnow (Upper Jeuno) and Yin Pocanakhu (Lower Jeuno).
  2. Get the Old Gauntlets key item from a Treasure Coffer in Crawlers' Nest.
  3. Meet Borghertz at the ??? in Port Jeuno.
  4. In Castle Zvahl Baileys, examine the Torch. It pops Dark Spark. Kill it, then check the torch again for Shadow Flames.
  5. Return to Borghertz.

AF Head & Body — Treasure Coffers

While you're on your Borghertz quest (or after it), on BST main: - Beast Jackcoat → Treasure Coffer in Beadeaux - Beast Helm → Treasure Coffer in Garlaige Citadel

PRO TIP — Farm coffer keys with /THF for Treasure Hunter, or hire a THF to pick locks.

AF +1

Sagheera (Port Jeuno) upgrades AF to +1 with Limbus materials. For example, Beast Helm +1 = Beast Helm + Limbus parts + ancient beastcoins.
