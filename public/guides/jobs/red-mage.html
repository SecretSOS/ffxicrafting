HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Most Red Mage guides on the web describe the game after 2009: Composure, Saboteur, Temper, Gain spells, Refresh II, Distract and Frazzle, a Scholar subjob, and 99-era melee builds. None of that is on Phoenix. This guide uses Phoenix's own server code (github.com/phoenixffxi/Phoenix) with its era-revert modules applied. Spell levels, enfeebling and enhancing formulas, trait values, skill caps, gear stats and drops all come from that code. Quest walkthroughs were checked against BG Wiki, because the server code can't tell you where to stand or what an NPC says.

Look for these boxes:

  • PRO TIP — technique that saves time, MP or gil
  • WARNING — a common way to die or waste gil
  • MONEY MAKER — gil on the side
  • FROM THE CODE — mechanics read straight from Phoenix's scripts
  • DUELIST'S CHALLENGE — optional, harder goals
  • CHECKPOINT — what you should have before moving on

WARNING — three things this guide can't settle from the code.

  • Slow and Paralyze potency use the 2014–2019 formulas from the Japanese wiki, which Phoenix keeps. They cap where the 2007 spells did (Slow ≈ 29%), but the curve below the cap is the modern one.
  • Merit spell ranks (Slow II, Paralyze II, Phalanx II) were reworked by Phoenix so rank 1 only unlocks the spell. The extra ranks add potency and magic accuracy on top. The exact numbers are in Chapter 5.
  • Live server settings such as resting MP and the enmity caps aren't public. The numbers here are the code defaults.

CHAPTER 1

MEET THE RED MAGE

Half white, half black, all of it on a timer.

On Phoenix a RDM does four things, in this order of importance:

  1. Refresh the mages. Refresh is 3 MP per tick for 2½ minutes. Your BLM, WHM and BRD keep casting because of you.
  2. Enfeeble. Slow, Paralyze, Dia, Blind and Gravity on everything. Enfeebling is your A+ skill, the best in the game.
  3. Haste the melee. Haste is about 15% faster attacks, separate from their gear haste.
  4. Back up the healer. Cure IV, Raise, Stoneskin and Phalanx when the White Mage is busy.

40 Convert 10:00 Swaps your current HP and MP. You can't use it with 0 MP

FROM THE CODE — what Convert really does. Convert sets your HP to your current MP and your MP to your current HP (scripts/globals/job_utils/red_mage.lua). With 900 HP and 200 MP you end up with 200 HP and 900 MP. Use it at full HP, then Cure yourself.

WARNING — Convert leaves you nearly dead. Your HP drops to whatever MP you had. Stand away from the mob, and have a Cure macro ready.

BECOMING A RED MAGE

No quest needed. Pick it at character creation.

Red Mage is a starting job. Choose it when you make your character, or change to it at any Mog House.

Your first shopping list

FROM THE CODE — the random durations. Phoenix reverts Blind, Paralyze and Silence to their old random durations (modules/phoenix/lua/globals/spells/enfeebling_spell.lua). A Paralyze can wear in 30 seconds or last 2 minutes. Recast when it wears, not on a timer.

PRO TIP — MND is Slow. Slow tops out at 29.2% when your MND is 75 points over the mob's. You'll never get that far on a high-level mob, so every point of MND gear is Slow potency. Same for Paralyze.

Elemental staves (the RDM's best purchase)

Every elemental staff adds +10 magic accuracy and +5% damage per point of its element bonus (magic_hit_rate.lua, damage_spell.lua). NQ staves are +2 (+20 accuracy, +10% damage); HQ staves are +3 (+30 accuracy, +15% damage).

MONEY MAKER — build them yourself. Every NQ elemental staff is a Woodworking 75 synth (Ebony Lumber + that element's bead); the HQ is the HQ result. If you level Woodworking, they pay for your RDM.

Chainspell

Sixty seconds of instant casting with no recast. Use it to: - Save a wipe: Cure IV, Cure IV, Raise, Raise, Raise. - Kill something fast: every nuke you have, back to back. - Lock down an NM: Sleep, Bind, Gravity, Paralyze, Slow, Blind in ten seconds.

CHAPTER RECAP

  • Refresh is +3 MP/tick. Haste is +14.65% on top of gear haste.
  • Slow and Paralyze run on MND. Bind, Gravity, Sleep and Blind run on INT.
  • Elemental staves: +30 magic accuracy for HQ. Buy them.

PRO TIP — what the report tells you. Bats in Garlaige Citadel shrug off Sleep, Colibri and Puks in the Aht Urhgan zones shrug off Gravity and Silence, and Mount Zhayolm's Ebony Puddings resist everything. Bracket by bracket

Levels 1–10 — home nation. Melee with a sword, Dia on every pull, Cure when you need it. Learn to cast Paralyze on anything that hits hard.

Levels 10–20 — Valkurm Dunes, Buburimu Peninsula, Korroloka. Fast Cast I at 15. You're the party's enfeebler and second healer: Dia and Paralyze on the target, Cure II on the tank. Enspells from 16; they're solo tools for now.

Levels 18–20 — subjob. Do The Old Lady or Elder Memories. Level WHM to 10 for parties; BLM or NIN later.

Levels 20–30 — Qufim, Yhoator and Yuhtunga jungles, Bibiki Bay. Gravity at 21 and Sleep at 25: you're the crowd control now. Holy Phial (MND+3, GeDha Evileye NM in Beadeaux) at 26.

PRO TIP — Race Specific Equipment (RSE) at level 25. Every race has its own four-piece set (Lv27–33) from The Goblin Tailor (Guttrix, Lower Jeuno). You can only get it during your race's 7 h 41 m "week", in one of three dungeons, with that dungeon's chest key — so farm the key before your week starts. For Red Mages: Hume Slacks/Pants (MND+3, MP+32), Elvaan Gauntlets (MP+24) and Ledelsens (MP+20), Mithra Gauntlets (MND+2) and Loincloth (MP+32), Galka Surcoat and Bracers (MP+32 each). Tarutaru pieces add HP, not caster stats. Full walkthrough: Race Specific Equipment guide. Live race and dungeon schedule: Phoenix RSE calendar.

Levels 30–40 — Garlaige Citadel, Eastern Altepa, Eldieme Necropolis. Dia II at 31, Phalanx at 33, Stoneskin at 34. Solid Wand (INT+5 MND+5, Woodworking 32 HQ). Bats don't sleep; use Bind or Gravity on adds instead.

Levels 40–50 — Crawlers' Nest, Attohwa Chasm, Gustav Tunnel. Refresh at 41 — you're now the most-wanted job in every party. Convert at 40. AF1 at 40 for the Fencing Degen. Haste at 48, Cure IV at 48. Magic Cuisses (INT+3 MND+3, King Arthro in Jugner Forest, always) and Crimson Blade (INT+5 MP+10, Roc in Sauromugue Champaign, always).

WARNING — Refresh order. Refresh the Black Mage and the White Mage first, then yourself, then anyone else who casts. Haste goes on the tank and the main damage dealer. Keep a list of timers: Refresh lasts 2:30, Haste 3:00.

Levels 50–60 — Quicksand Caves, Kuftal Tunnel, ToAU jungles. AF2 and AF3. Elemental staves at 51 (Chapter 5). Fast Cast III at 55. Shaman's Cloak (INT+4, MP+25, Centurio X-I in Quicksand Caves). Royal Cloak (Refresh+1, Clothcraft 70 HQ) at 59 for resting and idling; it blocks your head slot.

Levels 60–70 — Kuftal Tunnel, Mamook, Caedarva Mire. Protect IV at 63, Shell IV at 68. Mythic Wand (INT+8 MND+8, Woodworking 84) at 65. Enfeebling Torque (Enfeebling +7, BCNM loot) at 65. Ogre Ledelsens +1 (MND+5) at 70.

Levels 70–75 — Mount Zhayolm, Caedarva Mire, Lufaise Meadows. Errant / Mahatma pieces (big INT and MND, crafted) at 72. Jet Seraweels (INT+7 MND+7, Jaded Jody in Wajaom Woodlands). Savage Blade quest at Sword 240 (level 73). Spend merits on Slow II, Paralyze II and Phalanx II.

CHECKPOINT — level 75

  • Enfeebling at 276, Enhancing at 256.
  • HQ Earth, Ice and Dark staves at minimum.
  • Merit spells unlocked (Chapter 8).

NOT ON PHOENIX (DON'T FARM THESE)

WARNING — these show up in "best RDM gear" lists, but you can't get them here.

  • Zeni Notorious Monster and Nyzul Isle drops — Phoenix hides the ZNM pop NPCs, the Nyzul Isle NPC and the spawn points (modules/phoenix/lua/zones/npc_visibility.lua). That also blocks the Murgleis mythic and Death Blossom.
  • Einherjar rewards — the Einherjar NPC is hidden, so Odin's abjurations don't drop either.
  • Goliard pieces — in the database, but no drop, quest or shop on Phoenix gives them.
  • Nashira pieces — Salvage gear. Phoenix has no Salvage; the only source in the code is a later (Rhapsodies) vendor.
  • Snow Ring / Aqua Ring — Goldsmithing 102 recipes; the era craft cap is 100.

DUELIST'S CHALLENGE — Dalmatica. Kill Nidhogg or Aspidochelone for an Aquarian Abjuration: Body, craft the Cursed Dalmatica, and you have Refresh+1 with your head slot free for Duelist's Chapeau: two Refresh pieces at once.

CHAPTER 8

QUESTS, AF AND ENDGAME

The Warlock's set is built for casting. Get every piece.

AF1 · The Crimson Trial (level 40) → Fencing Degen

  1. Talk to Sharzalion in Southern San d'Oria as a level 40+ Red Mage.
  2. Go to Davoi. Zoning in while on the quest spawns Purpleflash Brukdok, a notorious Orc. Kill it for the Davoi Storage Key (it always drops).
  3. Trade the key to the Storage Hole in Davoi for the Orcish Dried Food key item.
  4. Return to Sharzalion.

WARNING — Purpleflash spawns on zone-in. It appears as soon as you enter Davoi on the quest. Have your party with you when you zone, not waiting outside.

AF2 · Enveloped in Darkness (level 50) → Warlock's Boots

  1. Talk to Curilla in Chateau d'Oraguille as a level 50+ Red Mage who has finished AF1. You get the Old Pocket Watch.
  2. Trade a Square of Velvet Cloth to Pagisalis in Northern San d'Oria for the Old Boots.
  3. In Crawlers' Nest, open a Treasure Chest with a Nest Chest Key (drops from the crawlers there) to get the Crawler Blood.
  4. Check the ??? (the digging spot) in Crawlers' Nest to bury the boots and blood.
  5. Come back after midnight Japan time (JST) and check the ??? again.

FROM THE CODE — the JST midnight wait. Phoenix restores the old wait: the boots are ready at the next JST midnight (7 a.m. Pacific, 8 a.m. during daylight saving), not after 30 seconds (modules/era/lua/quests/sandoria/enveloped_in_darkness.lua).

AF3 · Peace for the Spirit (level 50) → Warlock's Chapeau

  1. Talk to Curilla (Chateau d'Oraguille) after AF2.
  2. Talk to Sharzalion (Southern San d'Oria).
  3. Enter Fei'Yin. Miser Murphy spawns when you zone in; kill it for the Antique Coin.
  4. Trade the coin to the Dry Fountain in Fei'Yin.
  5. Talk to Sharzalion again, then Daggao (Southern San d'Oria).
  6. In Garlaige Citadel, check the Oaken Box to spawn the Guardian Statue. Kill it for the Nail Puller, then trade the Nail Puller to the Oaken Box.
  7. Zone into Northern San d'Oria with a free inventory slot. You get the Warlock's Chapeau and the title Paragon of Red Mage Excellence.

Borghertz's Vermillion Hands (level 50, after AF2) → Warlock's Gloves

  1. Talk to Guslam in Upper Jeuno as a level 50+ Red Mage who has finished Enveloped in Darkness.
  2. Open a Treasure Coffer in The Eldieme Necropolis for the Old Gauntlets.
  3. Report to Guslam. On your first Borghertz quest you then talk to Deadly Minnow (Upper Jeuno) and pay Yin Pocanakhu (Lower Jeuno) 1,000 gil; later Borghertz quests skip this.
  4. Check the ??? in Port Jeuno, then kill Dark Spark at the Torch in Castle Zvahl Baileys and check the Torch for Shadow Flames.
  5. Return to the ??? in Port Jeuno.

Savage Blade · Old Wounds

  1. Talk to Curilla (Chateau d'Oraguille) with Sword skill 240+ (level 73). You get the Weapon Training Guide and a Sapara of Trials.
  2. Earn 300 weapon skill points with the Sapara (5 per WS, +2/+4/+6 for skillchain levels 1/2/3).
  3. Trade the Sapara to Curilla for the Map to the Annals of Truth.
  4. In Quicksand Caves, check the ??? to spawn Girtablulu. Kill it, then check the ??? for the Annals of Truth.
  5. Return to Curilla.

FROM THE CODE — no merit weapon skills. Phoenix's weapon skill merit category has no upgrades, so Savage Blade is the top Red Mage weapon skill.
