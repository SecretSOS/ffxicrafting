A dual-craft path from 1 to 100 on Phoenix

Frog Lure in Giddeus covers Dark Bass all the way to Red Terrapin. No gear swap needed — Carbon Rod the whole way.
Don't sell your guild key items — you cannot rebuy them. If you drop Woodworking's Lumberjack key item, those recipes are locked forever on that character.
Millioncorn from Benaige in Southern San d'Oria is 48g, not 240g guild price. Cornstarch is vendor trash but cheap to make — float alchemy levels with it.
Guild shops restock every Vana'diel day (57.6 real seconds). If the NPC is sold out, wait or zone and come back.

Example: Putting It All Together

How a real guide section looks when you combine components

Fishing 30–40 Giddeus to Selbina — the Carbon Rod era
Frog Lure in Giddeus — Dark Bass to Red Terrapin, no gear swap needed. Carbon Rod the whole way.

Head to Giddeus with a Carbon Rod and Frog Lures. Dark Bass start biting at skill 30 and you'll transition to Red Terrapin around 36. Both sell to NPCs for decent gil, making this stretch self-funding.

Watch your inventory — Dark Bass don't stack. Bring a Mog Satchel or plan to make multiple trips.
