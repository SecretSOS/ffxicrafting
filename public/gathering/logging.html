Logging

Logging point drops by zone. Chance shows the per-swing probability of obtaining each item. Tool: Hatchet.

Logging lets you chop logs and harvest wood from designated logging points in forested zones. Equip a Hatchet and target a logging point (a tree stump or marked tree) to swing at it. Each swing has a chance to break the hatchet, and each point disappears after a set number of successful swings. Logging points respawn on a timer. The logs and lumber obtained are essential materials for Woodworking recipes, and some zones yield rare woods that are highly valued for high-level crafts.
