Excavation

Excavation point drops by zone. Chance shows the per-swing probability of obtaining each item. Tool: Pickaxe.

Excavation lets you dig up bones, fossils, and buried artifacts from designated excavation points found in specific zones like Attohwa Chasm, Maze of Shakhrami, and the Korroloka Tunnel. Equip a Pickaxe and target an excavation point to swing at it. Each swing has a chance to break the pickaxe. Excavation points yield different items from mining points even when using the same tool. Excavated materials are commonly used in Bonecraft and sometimes Alchemy.
