How much the fish heals when you miss. Higher = fish recovers more HP = harder fight. Lower is better.
Timer fishTime
Base catch time limit (seconds). Reduced by 10s on size mismatch. Higher = more time to land the catch.
Rank minRank–maxRank
Fish skill range the rod handles. Fish outside this window = higher snap and lose chance.
Size
Small or large. Must match the fish's size type or you lose time and risk breaking the rod.
