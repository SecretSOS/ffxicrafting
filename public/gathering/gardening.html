Gardening

Plant a seed in a flowerpot, feed it crystals, and harvest the result. Two-crystal seeds (herb, tree cuttings, tree saplings, wildgrass) accept two crystals fed over time; single-crystal seeds only use one.

Gardening is done inside your Mog House using flowerpots. Buy a flowerpot from the Mog House furnishing NPC, place it in your house, and plant a seed. Over real-world time your plant will grow through several stages. Feeding it elemental crystals during growth determines what it produces at harvest. Different seed and crystal combinations yield different items — the tables below show every possible outcome with exact weights. Gardening is a popular way to obtain crafting materials, rare seeds, and elemental ores without leaving town. You can tend up to 10 flowerpots across your Mog House and storage, making it a reliable passive income source.
