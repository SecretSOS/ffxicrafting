The Harvest Festival

Trick-or-treating in the three nations, Phoenix edition

Every October the merchants of San d'Oria, Bastok and Windurst put on monster costumes and wait for adventurers to bring them sweets. Trade a sweet to one of them and you'll either get a piece of event gear, get turned into a monster yourself, or just get thanked. The event gives out eight items: two hats and two staves, each with an upgraded version.

This guide is based on Phoenix's own server code (phoenixffxi/Phoenix, beta branch), not on retail wikis. Phoenix runs what the code calls the 2005 edition of the festival. Most guides you'll find online describe the 2007-or-later version (event stalls, Wake of the Lilies, bomb decorations, Pitchforks), and most of that isn't on Phoenix.

Check that it's actually running. The public Phoenix code ships with the festival turned off (HALLOWEEN_2005 = 0). The live server's settings aren't public, so we can't confirm from code that Phoenix will turn it on. The quickest test is in game: after the start date, walk past Faustin in Bastok Mines (J-9). If he's in a monster costume, the event is live. If the event is off, trading a sweet does nothing and you keep the sweet.
Chapter 1

How Trick-or-Treating Works

One sweet, one trade, one of three outcomes

Step 1 Stock up on sweets

Any sweet on the accepted list works, and they all give the same odds. The cheapest by far are cookies from Ness Rugetomal in Windurst Waters (F-10), who is also one of the trick-or-treat NPCs. Buy a stack and you can trade them right there.

Step 2 Free an inventory slot

The NPC only hands out an item if you have at least one empty slot. With a full bag, the item roll is skipped and the sweet is still used up.

Step 3 Trade one sweet to a costumed NPC

Trade a single sweet. All 22 NPCs work exactly the same way, so use whichever is closest. Repeat until you have everything you want.

Trick Staff II is the prize. It's a free, reusable Warp for any job at Lv. 1, with 10 charges and a one-hour cooldown. On a fresh server where Warp scrolls and Black Mage warps are scarce, that saves real gil and time. Get it on every character.
Phase 1 Collect the four base items

With no event gear equipped, each trade has a 1 in 3 chance to give you a base item you don't already own. The server never gives a duplicate: items in your Mog Safe and other storage count as owned.

Take off finished gear. If you're wearing an event head or staff whose upgrade you already have, the server checks that item, finds nothing to give, and stops. You get no item at all, and base-item drops are blocked until you take it off.
Phase 2 Upgrade by wearing the base item

Equip a base item and trade sweets. Each trade then has a 1 in 3 chance to give you its upgrade. You keep the base item. You can wear a hat and a staff at the same time: the server upgrades the hat first, then the staff on later trades, so you don't need to swap gear in between.

Chapter 5

Costumes

Turn into a beastman or monster for an hour

When a trade doesn't give an item, you may be turned into a monster instead. The costume is picked at random from eight looks: Yagudo, Quadav, Orc, Shade, Ghost, Hound, Skeleton or Dark Stalker. The beastman and Shade looks also have random variants. It lasts one hour, and you can remove it early by clicking off the Costume status icon.

Each sweet type gives a costume only once per character. The server remembers every sweet type you've traded, and there's a comment in the code saying this should reset each game day, but it never does. Once you've traded an Acorn Cookie, further Acorn Cookies can still give items, but they'll never give a costume again. To get more costumes, trade a different sweet.
Want costumes for screenshots? Save one each of several cheap sweets (Acorn Cookie, Cinna-cookie, Ginger Cookie, Goblin Chocolate, Dried Date, Baked Apple). Each new type gets one costume chance.
The code includes a hint line ("If you put this on and walk around, something...unexpected might happen...") meant for certain costume-and-zone combinations, which hints at the retail Pitchfork. On Phoenix it can never trigger: the check compares the zone's name to a zone number, so it never matches.
Chapter 6

Recommended Route

All 8 items in about 15 minutes

1 Get to Windurst Waters (F-10)

Clear several inventory slots and unequip your main hand and head. Buy 40+ Acorn Cookies from Ness Rugetomal, or bring your own sweets from home.

2 Collect the four base items

With nothing in your head or main slots, trade one cookie at a time to Ness Rugetomal until you have Pumpkin Head, Pumpkin Head II, Trick Staff and Trick Staff II. Expect about 14 cookies.

3 Pumpkin Head + Trick Staff

Equip both and keep trading. Horror Head comes first, then Treat Staff. About 6 cookies.

4 Pumpkin Head II + Trick Staff II

Swap to the II versions and trade until you have Horror Head II and Treat Staff II. About 6 cookies.

5 Unequip everything and stash it

You're done. Put the gear in storage (all eight are Rare/Ex, so you can't sell or trade them), but keep Trick Staff II handy for its warp.

Don't melee with the Treat Staff during the festival unless you want to go home. It has a 10% chance per swing to warp you to your Home Point.
